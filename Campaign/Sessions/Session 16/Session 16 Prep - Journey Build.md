# Session 16 Prep: The Sweet Road and the Thorn Under It

**Theme:** Fanewick shows its face first, then its teeth.  
**Setting:** Early **Sunnar** morning, hours after the hunters withdrew at Riverwalker camp. The party travels grove-ward toward [[Whitefeather Circle]] and [[Village of Mysteria]], but does not arrive this session.

> [!QUOTE] Trail-stone saying, reed-carved
> "The road smells sweetest where something has already bled into the roots."

---

| Beat | Time | Checkpoint Goal |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Dawn on the Sweet Road | 15 min | Establish Sunnar beauty and route unease |

| 4. First Wrongness | 20 min | Confirm taint + ritual + extraction overlap |

Act I (Beats 1-3): wonder under strain  
Act II (Beats 4-5): evidence and dread

**Run Rule:** Keep linear pacing unless players hard-detour. If time compresses, shorten Beats 2 and 4 before cutting Beat 5 or Beat 6.

## NPC Dossiers (History, War View, Current Plan)

Use these as motivation engines, not exposition dumps. Most NPCs know fragments, not grand truth.

### Jessa (travelling knot-ranger)

- **History:** Apprentice route-keeper trained on upper and lower wayfinding knots before Alula route collapse.
- **War awareness:** Knows Haven patrol patterns and courier signs, but frames war as "who closes roads first."
- **View on conflict:** Distrusts both Haven force and rebel spectacle; values predictable passage above ideology.
- **Current plan:** Rebuild a safe low-route map network and keep children and migrants off specimen corridors.
- **If engaged deeply:** Shares two "quiet paths" and one false marker used to mislead armed trackers.

### Old Varrek (elder trail pragmatist)

- **History:** Former toll witness at seasonal crossings; remembers pre-collapse agreements between clans and traders.
- **War awareness:** Knows supply behavior, not command structure; believes wars are won by starving roads.
- **View on conflict:** Anti-crusade, pro-survival. Respects strength only when it protects dependents.
- **Current plan:** Move small family groups between crossing nodes without drawing attention.
- **If engaged deeply:** Can name which crossings are still neutral by custom rather than law.

### [[Tillo Shortscale]] (optional Ribbet factor)

- **History:** Ledger broker who balanced canopy credit between traders, smugglers, and shrine tithe routes.
- **War awareness:** Understands market collapse mechanics better than military strategy.
- **View on conflict:** Believes both Haven and rebels exploit scarcity narratives.
- **Current plan:** Shift accounting to river ledgers and avoid any fixed market center.
- **If engaged deeply:** Can expose price manipulation and identify forged assay chains.

### Elder Merrow (Clan of the Bitter Ash)

- **History:** Once a mourning officiant; converted rites into emergency anti-taint practice after repeated losses.
- **War awareness:** Hears of Haven and Fanewraith as distant powers; experiences them as "creature theft and smoke."
- **View on conflict:** Believes outsiders make ruin and locals pay in blood.
- **Current plan:** Keep thorn spread contained through rites, preserve clan cohesion, avoid outside rule.

### Acolyte Pell (Bitter Ash novice)

- **Current plan:** Quietly collect proof that some beasts can be contained without immediate killing.

- **War awareness:** Understands command ladders enough to know quotas are policy, not accident.
- **View on conflict:** Views war as employment trap where refusal means hunger and replacement.

### [[Sylva the Trapper]]

- **War awareness:** Medium; knows less politics, more retaliation patterns.
- **View on conflict:** Personalized and emotional. Reads conflict through humiliation, fear, and vengeance.

- **If engaged deeply:** Might accept a face-saving compromise, but rarely first.

- **War awareness:** Knows enough to see contradiction between Kreil shadow orders and Phylax extraction demand.

## Challenge Lens (Run Philosophy)

Use this lens for every scene challenge:

- **Who is under pressure right now?** Name the person, creature, or system paying the cost.
- **What is unstable?** Make the instability visible (route, body, belief, supply, weather, ritual).
- **When does it get worse?** Add a clock (minutes, sunset, witnesses, patrol return, taint spread).
- **Where is leverage?** Place one concrete interaction point (rope knot, ledger, channel gate, social fault line).
- **Why should the party care?** Tie each challenge to one PC value plus one campaign consequence.

Success should solve an immediate problem and reveal a deeper one. Failure should move the story forward with cost, not dead-end the session.

## Beat 1 - Dawn on the Sweet Road (~15 min)

**Scene Type:** Travel | Atmosphere  
**Checkpoint:** Establish sensory baseline and first tension seam.

### Challenge Anatomy (Who / What / When / Where / Why)

- **Who:** The road itself, plus displaced travelers who are no longer visible but leave marks.
- **What:** Route certainty is breaking; old pilgrim logic and new specimen logic overlap.
- **When:** First travel hour, before the party can settle into autopilot.
- **Where:** Split-track segment between Riverwalker range and grove-ward shelf paths.
- **Why:** This beat teaches the party that "just traveling" is now a strategic and moral act.

### Scenarios and Stakes

- **Scenario A - Careful read:** Party slows, reads tracks, and chooses safer path.
- **Scenario B - Push pace:** Party ignores detail, arrives faster, pays stress and misses context.
- **Scenario C - Track hunters/specimens:** Party pursues route clues to identify who else uses corridor.

### Why Party Engages / What It Solves

- Solves immediate navigation confidence and helps avoid avoidable attrition.
- Establishes that extraction pressure remains active beyond last session's parley.
- Gives each PC an early agency touchpoint before heavier moral scenes.

### What They Can and Cannot Learn

- **Can find:** Second transport corridor evidence, directional smoke clue, old Haven trace.
- **Cannot confirm yet:** Who currently commands corridor, whether hunters returned, or if route leads to lab, cult, or broker handoff.

### Read-Aloud (short)

> [!QUOTE]
> Morning does not break so much as gather weight. Pollen sticks to the tongue, sap shines on bark, and nectar hangs too sweet in the air. Sunrise vines climb while you watch. For a breath, the wind carries rope-smoke from behind you. Ahead, the forest opens.

### Read-Aloud (long, optional)

> [!QUOTE]
> Ikla's painted sky has not shifted since before sleep, but the road has. Dew clings to every leaf like glass. Insects drone in thick, layered sound. A deer with bark-ridged fur watches from the brush and does not flinch.  
> Your boots follow old game turns and pilgrim marks cut into lichen, but between them sit wheel-ruts that do not belong to last night's hunters. The day smells of flowers and growth, yet one sharp line of tar and burned hemp cuts through once, then vanishes.

### Senses

- **Sight:** Painted gradient sky, hyper-bright dew, fearless bark-ridged deer.
- **Sound:** Insect blanket, distant river, one hawk cry with wrong echo.
- **Smell:** Nectar, pollen, warm sap, and brief tarred rope smoke.
- **Touch:** Damp heat, sticky leaf-film, straps already wet.
- **Taste:** Honey-thick air with faint metallic note if Lyra memory is active.

### Information and Skill Delivery

- **Instinct DC 11** or **Knowledge DC 12** reads split route: pilgrim trace vs game trail.
- Older cage-ruts indicate a second specimen corridor.
- Growth without reaping is visible and immediate.

### Fear Usage

- **Low:** Deer holds one PC in eye contact too long.
- **Medium:** Pollen burst; forced pace without water inflicts **1 Stress**.
- **High:** Not used in this beat.

### Player Spotlights

- **Fianna:** Owl-scrape on bark, warning or invitation.
- **Fern:** Fungril hum strengthens toward grove route.
- **Sabriel:** Haven heel-marks overlain by Wickling knots.
- **Snicket:** Rut spacing suggests disciplined transport, not panic flight.
- **Gnirth:** Stream clarity feels unnaturally controlled.

### GM Variations

- **Cautious table:** Give safe marker immediately, keep tension in atmosphere.
- **Aggressive table:** Trigger pollen stress early to force pacing choice.
- **Sabriel-forward:** Add a half-erased Haven boot-mark code near split.

### Transition

"The trail narrows, sweetness turns heavy, and something ahead has grown faster than the world can sustain."

## Beat 2 - The Living Cathedral (~25 min)

**Scene Type:** Exploration | Skill Challenge  
**Checkpoint:** Show wonder becoming ecological danger without default combat.

### Sacred Site Frame: The Green Cathedral of Nikta's Lower Eye

Treat this as a true nature-cathedral, not just a dangerous biome.

- **Who built it:** No single builder. It was shaped over generations by Wickling caretakers called the **Lantern Sowers**, who trained vines and branch arches rather than cutting stone.
- **Primary divinity:** **Nikta, the Great Owl**, especially the aspect locals called *the Lower Eye* (the eye that watches mortal cycles of sowing and reaping).
- **Secondary devotions:** Quiet offerings to **Bolgin** for threshold wisdom and to local Faints for pollinator balance.
- **Who worshipped here:** Wickling route-keepers, seasonal gatherers, and families before long journeys; occasionally Riverwalker mediators.
- **What changed:** Since Nikta's cycle wound and regional disruption, the site continues to "sow" but fails to "reap," turning blessing mechanics into overgrowth pressure.

### Old Customs (before current corruption)

- **Dawn hush:** Entrants crossed in silence until the first owl-call.
- **Five-gift rite:** One gift per sense (seed, water, feather-thread, sap-salt, and breath-prayer).
- **No iron inside ring:** Metal was wrapped or left at outer roots except for emergency tools.
- **Balanced taking:** Harvesters removed one growth for every two cuttings that opened light.
- **Departure mark:** Travelers tied one reed-knot to mark safe passage for the next party.

### Player Discoveries (bullet clues)

- A root altar with five weathered offering hollows matching the old five-gift rite.
- Reed-knots at shoulder height marking safe lanes now half-swallowed by new growth.
- An owl-mask carved in living bark with one eye overgrown shut (Nikta wound symbol).
- A discarded iron-wrap bundle outside the old sacred boundary, confirming no-iron custom.
- A faded caretaker tally stick: "two cuttings for one taking" scratched in old Wickling shorthand.
- Pollinator stones arranged in a spiral now broken by black-veined vine intrusion.

### What the party can gain by engaging the cathedral as sacred space

- **Knowledge framing:** Realizes this is a collapsed stewardship system, not random nature violence.
- **Social leverage:** Respectful handling gives advantage with tradition-minded Wicklings later.
- **Mechanical edge:** Identifying old safe-lane knots can reduce one future travel hazard.

### What they cannot restore tonight

- They cannot re-establish the full sow/reap cycle.
- They cannot clear the cathedral permanently without a larger Nikta-thread intervention.

### Challenge Anatomy (Who / What / When / Where / Why)

- **Who:** Local fauna and the party; both are trapped in an overgrown system.
- **What:** Growth has outrun reaping, so ecology becomes pressure without intent.
- **When:** Mid-morning pollen peak; too early for fatigue excuses.
- **Where:** Former grazing corridor now transformed into dense vertical choke.
- **Why:** This is the campaign thesis in miniature: beauty and harm are no longer separable.

### Scenarios and Stakes

- **Scenario A - Harm minimization:** Protect creatures and party while preserving movement speed.
- **Scenario B - Research priority:** Harvest samples at greater short-term risk for long-term cure thread.
- **Scenario C - Tactical bypass:** Prioritize travel efficiency and defer moral intervention.

### Why Party Engages / What It Solves

- Solves immediate passage problem while signaling each PC's doctrine under pressure.
- Builds evidence that corruption is systemic, not isolated to cult or Haven sites.
- Seeds future remedy options (samples, pattern recognition, route memory).

### What They Can and Cannot Learn

- **Can find:** Safe lanes, sample viability, corridor shift linked to regional imbalance.
- **Cannot solve yet:** Root cause of Nikta wound effects or permanent stabilization method.

### Read-Aloud

> [!QUOTE]
> Trees lean inward like cathedral ribs. Sap beads every surface. Vines close every gap. A hare watches from its burrow with velvet antlers and calm, human-still eyes.  
> Then a bird that should be a sparrow perches at eye level, body stretched fox-long. It does not flee. The forest is not attacking. It is overflowing.

### Senses

- **Sight:** Mega-growth ferns, oversized songbirds, sunrise-vine tips browning.
- **Sound:** Loaded wood creak, distant horn with no return call.
- **Smell:** Peak sweetness with rot rising underneath.
- **Touch:** Spore dust coats skin and cloth.
- **Taste:** Sugar-sick air if party lingers.

### Skill Challenge (resolve 2-3 checks)

| Approach | Skill | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Read growth pattern | Knowledge | 12 | Safe line through overgrowth | Lost time and pollen **Stress** |
| Calm trapped fauna | Presence / Instinct | 13 | Free hare or redirect pressure growth | Panic attracts hazard |
| Harvest viable sample | Finesse / Knowledge | 13 | Brindle-relevant sample secured | Taint smear flares risk |
| Scout bypass | Agility / Instinct | 12 | Find route around reed hazard | Razor cuts + delay |

### Route Insert: Reedglass Bend (optional)

> [!QUOTE]
> Shoulder-high reeds flash wet as glass. Wind through them sounds like sharpened whisper.

- Edges cause shallow cuts on contact.
- Split wagon marks show old Alula supply branch.
- Choice: follow marks or climb pilgrim shelf.

### Fear Usage

- **Low:** Vine tracks one PC's movement like a patient predator.
- **Medium:** Group pollen flash; 2-second Lyra fragment (missing owl eye).
- **High:** Mega-fern snaps on pack or limb; **Strength DC 13** to break free.

### Player Spotlights

- **Fianna:** Mercy decision on antlered hare.
- **Fern:** "Almost-healing" corrupted bloom temptation.
- **Gnirth:** Renewal impulse at still pool edge.
- **Snicket:** Elevated parallel trail scouting.
- **Sabriel:** Bodyguard beat under collapsing growth.

### GM Variations

- **Cautious table:** Keep to environmental challenge, no creature pressure.
- **Aggressive table:** Add one fleeing tainted straggler as chase bait only.
- **Sabriel-forward:** Broken Haven strap caught in vine, very recent.

### Transition

"By midday, sweetness turns rancid. Smoke finds you again, but not from ritual fire."

## Beat 6 - Challenge Fork (~25-35 min)

**Scene Type:** Moral / Skill / Social  
**Checkpoint:** One major choice with irreversible color.

Pick one primary fork and give each PC one decisive roll.

### Fork Selection Logic (GM)

- Pick the fork that best pays off the strongest emotional signal from Beats 3-5.
- If party energy is reflective, run **A** or **D**.
- If party energy is confrontational, run **B**.
- If party wants motion and rescue tension, run **C**.

Each fork should answer one question: "What kind of people are we when there is no clean good option?"

### Fork A - The Living Pen (default)

| Approach | Skill | DC |
|---|---|---:|
| Calm clan and hold line | Presence | 14 |
| Open pen quietly | Finesse | 13 |
| Argue Haven harms worse | Knowledge / Presence | 12 |
| Contain freed beast | Agility / Instinct | 13 |

**Stakes:** mercy now vs spread later vs social debt.

**Who / What / Why:** Merrow's circle, one living tainted beast, and the party's doctrine collide over immediate suffering vs future spread.

## Points of Interest: Histories, Current State, Interactions

Use these to make each location feel old, wounded, and still functional.

### The Sweet Road Split (Beat 1)

- **History:** Once a routine Riverwalker-to-grove feeder path, maintained by seasonal pruning crews and shrine keepers.
- **Current state:** Pruning lapsed, side channels widened, unofficial transport use increased.
- **If party interacts:** They can map safe/unsafe turns and detect non-local traffic rhythms.
- **What remains hidden:** Who currently controls secondary corridor scheduling.

**Old custom:** Travelers tied reed-knots on the "safe branch" before dawn departure.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Two knot styles layered on same branch (older civic knot, newer hunter utility knot).
- A faded waymarker prayer to Nikta asking for "balanced paths."
- Track-depth mismatch proving heavy traffic moved through recently despite local caution.

### The Living Cathedral Corridor (Beat 2)

- **History:** Sacred grove-chapel known as the **Green Cathedral of Nikta's Lower Eye**, tended by Lantern Sowers who shaped living arches instead of stone walls.
- **Divinity served:** Primarily **Nikta** (cycle watch), with threshold petitions to **Bolgin**.
- **Worshippers:** Route-keepers, gatherers, families before seasonal journeys, and occasional mediators.
- **Customs:** Dawn silence, five-sense offering rite, no iron inside inner ring, "two cuttings for one taking."
- **Current state:** Reaping-cycle disruption causes overgrowth and behavior distortion; the site still "sows" but cannot complete the cycle.
- **If party interacts:** They can extract viable sample and route around worst density.
- **What remains hidden:** Whether imbalance started local-first or was amplified by outside stressors.

**Player discoveries:**
- Owl-mask bark carving with one eye swallowed by vine growth.
- Five offering hollows at root altar, each tied to one sense.
- Old caretaker tally stick describing balanced-cut ritual now ignored.

### Reedglass Bend (Beat 2 optional)

- **History:** Old caution route used in wet seasons when high paths failed.
- **Current state:** Reeds are sharper and more brittle due altered water chemistry and neglect.
- **If party interacts:** They can find old wagon branch and trace former Alula supply relation.
- **What remains hidden:** Which faction last used branch for live transport.

**Old custom:** River-crosser's whisper - speak a name softly before entering reeds so the next traveler is remembered.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Reed bundles tied in memorial pairs where crossings once claimed lives.
- Tool-marks from recent cutting done by trained hands, not refugees.

### Crossing Node (Beat 3)

- **History:** Seasonal toll and map exchange point connecting high market routes to lower river lines.
- **Current state:** Informal reroute politics, trust scarcity, children/families in transit.
- **If party interacts:** They can gain route truth, social goodwill, and rumor quality.
- **What remains hidden:** Which broker network now profits from reroute choke points.

**Old custom:** Crossing witness rite - elders repeated destination names aloud to prevent map-fraud.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Toll-stone grooves filled with fresh wax where names used to be scored.
- Cancelled chalk marks proving at least one smuggler circuit was intentionally shut.
- A half-burned courier scrap stamped with a centuria hand-sign.

### Abandoned Waystation (Beat 4)

- **History:** Merchant rest stop with assay and bloom-handling function.
- **Current state:** Service collapse left mixed evidence and opportunistic reuse.
- **If party interacts:** They can build forensic narrative from overlapping signatures.
- **What remains hidden:** Full command chain linking field quotas to upper directives.

**Old custom:** Merchants marked "clean sleep" circles using ash and salt before closing fires.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Two ash chemistries in one pit (ritual blend vs charred hemp residue).
- Broken assay slate with counts erased but pressure marks still readable.
- Thorn-tree hangpoint where Haven ribbon and Wickling offering shared a branch.

### Bitter Ash Rite Circle (Beat 5)

- **History:** Adapted from a funerary rite called **Last Warmth**, once used to guide dead kin, not living tainted beasts.
- **Divinity invoked:** Usually **Qui'Gar** for thorn-endings, sometimes **Hyacynis** for small mercies.
- **Who practiced it:** Marginal clans with limited healer access and repeated taint-loss cycles.
- **Customs (old form):** One bowl for name, one for ash, one for seed return. No living offerings.
- **Current state:** Rite distorted into emergency taint management doctrine.
- **If party interacts:** They can influence one rite outcome and social reputation memory.
- **What remains hidden:** Whether clan ideology can shift without external survival support.

**Player discoveries:**
- Three bowl arrangement misused compared to elder funerary layout.
- Young acolyte's private token pouch with unburned seed beads (doubt marker).
- Pen frame stamped with supplier marks from outside clan territory.

### Broken Weir Crossing (Fork D)

- **History:** Community-engineered flow control that supported fish movement and ford safety.
- **Current state:** Structural fatigue plus contamination burden and debris choke.
- **If party interacts:** They can restore temporary function and reduce immediate downstream harm.
- **What remains hidden:** Upstream source cluster strong enough to re-break patch in future.

**Old custom:** First-flow blessing - fishers poured first bucket back upstream before taking catch.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Rope replacement sections from three different craft traditions (multiple repair attempts).
- Debris composition proving both natural flood and burned settlement runoff.
- Flow marks showing when contamination pulse first intensified.

### Edge Camp Split Marker (Beat 7)

- **History:** Pilgrim decision marker where roads once implied pilgrimage, trade, and kin travel all at once.
- **Current state:** Marker now represents competing urgencies and diminished certainty.
- **If party interacts:** They can lock next-session route priority with shared intent.
- **What remains hidden:** Which choice will cost more in trust versus time.

**Old custom:** Travelers placed one pebble under chosen symbol and one under the path they refused, to acknowledge cost.  
**Player discoveries:**
- Fresh pebbles under both symbols from indecisive or split groups.
- Knife-scratched warnings layered over older blessing carvings.
- A single owl feather pinned with black thread, recent and deliberate.
