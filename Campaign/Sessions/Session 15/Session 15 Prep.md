## Beat 1 - Lyra's hut vision recap (10-15 min)

**Scene Type:** Social / Vision / Lore Delivery  
**Checkpoint:** Re-anchor lore that was rushed at Session 14 close.

### Read-Aloud (opening)

> [!QUOTE]
> The hut is smaller than memory and warmer than it should be. Smoke does not rise here. It coils. It waits.  
> Lyra asks no new questions tonight. She only nods once, feeds the brazier three pinches of dark herb, and the taste of iron returns to the back of your tongues.  
> "No catechism," she murmurs. "No lecture. Only what the smoke remembers that haste forgot."

### Vision chain (deliver continuously; pause only for breath)

1. **The Picnic Cloth**
   - A vast cloth pulled taut between unseen hands: one in steel, one in thorn, and a third stain that spreads without fingers.
   - Lyra's line: "You keep asking which hand is right. Ask what tears."

2. **Red Flowers, Double Debt**
   - The cloth becomes soil. Red flowers bloom in impossible density.
   - Their roots drink from two wells - one mortal, one spirit.
   - Each bloom leaves both wells lower; each field must grow larger to give the same cure.
   - Bridge line: "A cure that eats tomorrow is still a hunger."

3. **Broken Bodies, Missing Spirits**
   - Two colossal silhouettes: the granit ophidian and the bog tyrant, both hollow where spirit should live.
   - Their bodies remain; their cycles do not.
   - Bridge line: "When body and spirit divorce, the land keeps the debt."

4. **Nikta's Split Gaze**
   - Owl-shadow turns overhead; one eye sows, one eye should reap, but one is absent.
   - Spring keeps arriving and never leaves.
   - Roots knot; fruit overripens; carrion does not clear.
   - Bridge line: "Growth without ending is panic with flowers on it."

5. **Braid of Three**
   - Bloom, thorn, and night appear as masks braided on one cord.
   - The cord pulls south toward marsh and Spire-path.
   - A distant horn calls, and herds turn from old routes.
   - Bridge line: "If one throat sings every hymn, forests forget how to argue."

6. **Lyra's Cost**
   - A palm opens over river water. A silver thread leaves flesh and dissolves.
   - Lyra appears older each time she communes.
   - A second silhouette stands near the threshold (Fern-shaped, unresolved).
   - Final line: "That is the price of bringing spirit-fire into mortal cups."

### Read-Aloud (ending)

> [!QUOTE]
> The smoke tears like wet cloth. The hut walls return. Coals tick softly.  
> Lyra's voice is tired now. "Now we hear what came back from the treeline. Horn or blood. Either way, the sermon is over."

### Senses

- **Sight:** Copper bowl glow, smoke ribbons behaving like thought, Lyra's patchwork cloak catching ember-light.
- **Sound:** Coals ticking, distant river drumming, one far-off shout from camp as vision ends.
- **Smell:** Mint, damp bark, old ash, bitter-metal edge.
- **Touch:** Heat on shins, cool air at neck, pressure behind eyes during each transition.
- **Taste:** Metallic tang like blood on a coin.

### Information to Deliver

- Red flowers are a costly bridge between mortal/spirit realms.
- Shun'Aush and Bolgin are examples of cycle-breaking spirit-body severance.
- Returning Nikta's eyes restores the sow/reap hinge.
- Fanewraith's braid of influences points south toward Spire routes and marshlands.
- Lyra pays life-essence each communion; this cure model is not infinitely sustainable.

### Fear Usage

- **Low:** PCs briefly feel they cannot move when the Owl appears.
- **Medium:** One PC hears their own voice from the masks.
- **High:** Vision "stutter" repeats one image until a PC speaks a name aloud.

### Player Spotlights

- **Fianna:** Which image feels like Nikta's warning rather than Lyra's interpretation?
- **Fern:** What did the "second threshold" feel like in your body?
- **Sabriel:** Which part sounded like Haven doctrine turned inside out?
- **Snicket:** What in that vision looked like a supply chain rather than prophecy?
- **Gnirth:** Which image felt familiar in a way you cannot explain?

### Transition

"Before anyone can speak, a horn note splits the camp air - short, urgent, not alarm but near enough."

## Beat 2 - Camp freeplay (round two) (30-60 min)

**Scene Type:** Social / Exploration  
**Checkpoint:** Use missed content from Session 14 to enrich relationships before conflict.

Pick 3-5 beats below based on table interest:

### Optional camp beats

1. **Kesra at the water-slab**
   - She now cleanses three times daily; metallic taste stronger.
   - She shows body-map tattoos of river branches and where corruption now comes from the north.
   - Hook: "If this keeps climbing, we stop purifying and start triaging."

2. **Brenn's fire-circle**
   - He asks for one true line for a song about losses (Demetrius, Alula, or "unseen dead").
   - He jokes to hide fear about the unchanged sky palette.
   - Hook: "Songs are weather reports when no one trusts weather."

3. **Ferrick's pen and sick small beast**
   - A tainted scale-squirrel equivalent appears with black-vein pattern.
   - Compare this to Brindle's pattern and to what scouts later bring back.
   - Hook: "We shelter what we can. Cities inventory what they can."

4. **Silas and Sabriel (quiet thread)**
   - Silas speaks plainly on Tavore: conviction can harden into cruelty when grief chooses one lesson.
   - Hook: "Orders don't carry consequence. People do."

5. **Old Galapa prophecy fragment**
   - Replace rambling with one usable line:
   - "I dreamed the horn walked south and the cages walked north. Both were full."

6. **Knot-ranger teaching children**
   - Rope lesson mirrors choices: slip knot (quick), binding knot (secure), rescue knot (costly but safe).
   - Hook: symbolic foreshadowing for caged creature decision.

### Senses (camp baseline)

- **Sight:** Wet ropes steaming by fires, tattoos inked with river deltas, children moving around stacked barrels.
- **Sound:** Drum taps, knot instruction calls, birds going quiet whenever north wind shifts.
- **Smell:** Fish smoke, moss, iron-rich water.
- **Touch:** Damp wood, net cord fibers, heat from low braziers.
- **Taste:** Salt and metal in the same breath.

### Fear Usage

- **Low:** A child asks whether "tainted" means "evil."
- **Medium:** A healthy animal refuses clean water and drinks only from a shadowed puddle.
- **High:** Camp dogs all look north at once, silent.

### Transition

"A pair of scouts staggers into the clearing, one supporting the other, and something in a wrapped net twists between them."

## Beat 3 - Scout return trigger (10-15 min)

**Scene Type:** Social / Urgent Report  
**Checkpoint:** Deliver the unresolved scouting-party seed.

### Read-Aloud

> [!QUOTE]
> Mud to the knees, blood on sleeves, and one torn net drawn between them like a stretcher - the scouts arrive without ceremony.  
> Inside the net bundle is a tainted creature, small enough to hold, wrong enough to fear. Its veins move like spilled ink under skin.  
> "Cages," one scout says, breathing hard. "They were taking them alive. More than one wagon. We cut one loose. Couldn't hold the line."

### Core report points (short, punchy)

- Same hunter crew seen before (the trio) now backed by Haven guards.
- They claim official orders to take tainted creatures to upper-city labs for cure research.
- They also claimed to have seized creatures from a cult conducting sacrifices.
- Not all captures were cult-sourced; many were taken from wild populations too.

### Branching trigger

- **If party pursues now:** Move directly to Beat 4 at a nearby riverbend/cage-track site.
- **If party delays:** They get one more camp scene, then encounter occurs on departure road (same content, altered terrain).

### Transition

"Silas wipes blood from the scout's jaw and looks to the party. 'Now, or on the road - you decide where this argument happens.'"

## Beat 4 - Hunters and the moral dilemma (45-70 min)

**Scene Type:** Social Standoff / Optional Combat  
**Checkpoint:** High-stakes choice with no clean moral victory.

### Adversary setup (if combat erupts)

- [[Archer Guard]] as **Rorik**
- [[Masked Thief]] as **Sylva**
- [[Bladed Guard]] as **Tessa**
- 2-3 additional [[Bladed Guard]] Haven escorts (or Grenn/Dalla equivalents)
- Optional handler (non-frontline; flees if pressed)

```encounter
party: Main Party
creatures:
  - [Arched Guard, Rorik]
  - [Masked Thief, Sylvia]
  - [Bladed Guard, Tessa]
  - 4: Bladed Guard
```

### Encounter framing

Run as a parley with weapons visible. Combat should be a consequence, not default.

### Read-Aloud (arrival)

> [!QUOTE]
> The cage wagon sits where mud meets stone, wheels chocked, tarps tied down with military knots.  
> You recognize them before they recognize you - Rorik on the ridge with bow half-raised, Sylva at the latch, Tessa watching hands not faces.  
> This time they wear confidence borrowed from uniforms behind them.

### Lore drops to weave in dialogue

Use across multiple speakers, not one dump:

- **Same trio, new backing:** "Contract's back on. Haven paid for protection this time."
- **Haven order:** "Upper tiers need live samples. No samples, no cure."
- **Cult origin:** "We pulled some from ritual pits before they were burned for blessings."
- **Wild captures too:** "And yes, we trap what's out there. You don't cure a plague by guessing."

### Suggested dialogue barks

- **Rorik:** "Last time you called us monsters. How many sick did that save?"
- **Sylva:** "You free them, they spread. You cage them, maybe someone learns."
- **Tessa:** "I just want one decision that isn't made over a corpse."
- **Haven guard:** "Orders are specimen priority. Interference counts as obstruction."

### Moral options

1. **Free the creatures (force or sabotage):**
   - Riverwalkers approve.
   - Risk of taint spread rises.
   - Haven marks party as obstructionist.

2. **Allow transport to Haven labs:**
   - Potential cure progress.
   - Riverwalker trust strain.
   - Party complicit in coercive capture.

3. **Force compromise (hard social win):**
   - Split custody, tagged releases, supervised study, or one-creature exchange.
   - Requires strong Presence plan; best narrative complexity.

### Mechanics suggestions

- **Presence DC 13-15:** Hold ceasefire while terms are argued.
- **Instinct DC 12:** Detect who is lying by omission.
- **Knowledge DC 13:** Identify artificial taint signatures in bait/collars.
- **Finesse DC 12-14:** Open cage under pressure.
- **Agility DC 12:** Reposition creature safely during chaos.

### Fear Usage

- **Low:** Creature panics and bites through rope.
- **Medium:** Guard misreads a movement and triggers initiative.
- **High:** One cage latch snaps and multiple creatures break containment.

### Transition (choose by outcome)

- **If resolved peacefully:** "No one cheers. Everyone only checks what is still breathing."
- **If combat:** "The mud is churned black. Survivors count cages, then count each other."

## Beat 5 - Fallout and road south (20-30 min)

**Scene Type:** Consequence / Decision  
**Checkpoint:** Lock in social consequences and move campaign direction.

### Consequence matrix

| Party choice | Riverwalker reaction | Haven reaction | World consequence |
|--------------|----------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Free creatures | Warm trust, urgent concern | Hostile reports filed | More tainted movement in wild |
| Allow transport | Cooler trust, pragmatic silence | Cooperative channels open | Lab lead available, ethical stain remains |
| Compromise | Respect (if terms real) | Uneasy compliance | Partial data + partial sanctuary, future friction |

### Hook lines for departure

- **Silas:** "Spire first. Arguments later. If Fanewraith moves before you do, this all gets smaller and crueler."
- **Lyra:** "South is not safety. It is relevance."
- **Kesra:** "If you find why the north poisoned the river, send word before the next cleanse."

### Transition

"By nightfall the camp is quieter than it was this morning. By dawn, your packs are by the path."

## Beat 6 - Southbound travel toolkit (drop-in modules)

Use 2-4 this session; bank the rest for Session 16.

## Route site cards

### 1) Reedglass Bend

> [!QUOTE]
> Reeds rise shoulder-high, their edges shining like wet glass. Wind through them sounds like whispers being sharpened.

- **Wrongness:** Minor cuts on anything that brushes through.
- **Clue:** Cage-wheel marks turn here, then split.
- **Choice:** Follow wagon trail or higher pilgrim path.
- **Hook south:** One reed bundle tied with Haven assay ribbon.

### 2) Pilgrim Stones

> [!QUOTE]
> Owl-carved stones lean from the mud, old prayers worn smooth where hands once passed.

- **Wrongness:** One marker has been scraped and recarved with thorn sigils.
- **Clue:** Fresh boot scuffs and iron-nail chips.
- **Choice:** Restore marker (time cost) or press pace.
- **Hook south:** Marker points toward Spire shelf.

### 3) Salt-Lick Hollow

> [!QUOTE]
> The mud is cratered with hooves. The water tastes of pennies and old storm.

- **Wrongness:** Black-vein algae around hoof prints.
- **Clue:** Rohkin track overlays human drag marks.
- **Choice:** Aid weak herdling or maintain pursuit.
- **Hook south:** Herd direction and cage direction converge.

### 4) Ash Orchard

> [!QUOTE]
> Trees bloom red on bark split gray as burned bone. Petals fall warm.

- **Wrongness:** Out-of-season flowering tied to soil drain.
- **Clue:** Harvest cuts made recently by careful blades.
- **Choice:** Gather samples (risk taint) or leave untouched.
- **Hook south:** Confirms red-flower extraction route.

### 5) Broken Weir Crossing

> [!QUOTE]
> A fish gate lies snapped in two, rope and timber braided with black weed.

- **Wrongness:** Water purification no longer holds here.
- **Clue:** Riverwalker repair marks overrun by new contamination.
- **Choice:** Spend time to patch temporary flow or move on.
- **Hook south:** Pollution pulse strongest from northern tributary branch.

### 6) Night Bloom Shelf

> [!QUOTE]
> Flowers open in daylight and close in darkness, as if their nights have traded places.

- **Wrongness:** Divinity rhythm mismatch.
- **Clue:** Small ritual ash circles (cult presence).
- **Choice:** Disturb circle (risk reaction) or observe.
- **Hook south:** Symbols match reports of sacrifice cults.

### 7) Hinge Cairn

> [!QUOTE]
> Two stone bowls sit at a cairn's base. One is full of seed husks. The other is empty and cracked.

- **Wrongness:** Sowing bowl intact; reaping bowl absent.
- **Clue:** Nikta icon split in two.
- **Choice:** Leave offering for omen or keep moving.
- **Hook south:** Spiritual echo of stolen Eye.

### 8) Marshwatch Rookery

> [!QUOTE]
> Carrion birds circle a broken cage sunk in peat, ribbons fluttering from bent bars.

- **Wrongness:** No scavenger touches the carcass inside.
- **Clue:** Haven tag and cult braid both present.
- **Choice:** Burn remains or preserve evidence.
- **Hook south:** Confirms both groups are using the same corridors.

## Travel world-texture loops (pepper every 15-20 min)

- The sky keeps the same painted gradient Brenn complained about.
- River taste shifts from brine to blood-metal and back.
- Healthy animals avoid certain pools; tainted ones seek them.
- Distant horn calls answer each other with no visible herd.
- Tracks alternate: square military heels, then barefoot pilgrim prints.
- Wind carries incense one mile from nowhere.
- Insects cluster around red blooms but die before landing.

## GM options and variations

- **If party avoids scout lead:** Trigger hunter encounter on road near Reedglass Bend.
- **If party is exhausted:** Compress to vision -> one camp beat -> encounter -> departure.
- **If party over-negotiates:** Add deadline pressure (storm front, herd movement, sunset crossing).
- **If party goes full combat-first:** Ensure at least one source survives to deliver cult/Haven lore.
- **If party seeks "best outcome":** Offer compromise path but attach cost (time, faction trust, resource).
