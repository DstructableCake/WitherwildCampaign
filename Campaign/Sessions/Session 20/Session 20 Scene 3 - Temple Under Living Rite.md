# Scene 3: Temple Under Living Rite

*Scene Type:* Ritual Combat | Tactical Puzzle  
*Checkpoint:* Lane B

---

## Goal

Hold the ritual lane, protect Bound Voices, and act only on the right beat windows.

---

## Adversary Package

- Bodyguard: [[Fowlbear]] (guards Tavore and the anchors)
- Skulk: [[Doppelhound]]
- Minions: [[Cult Initiate]] ×2 (rite attendants)
- Leader: [[The Masked Commander]] — command first; add her full Leader sheet (3 BP) when PCs press her or the rite needs a spike
- Optional reinforce: one [[Bladed Guard]] if the first exchange goes too clean

---

## Read-Aloud Cue

> [!QUOTE]
> Green-white flame breathes in a rhythm older than speech. Chains of root-light bind three living voices to carved stone.  
> Tavore's mask turns toward you. "Late, as ever," she says.  
> At her flank, feathered bear-mass and a goose-head wait like a living wall. Robed attendants clutch ritual knives at the anchors. Beside them, another beast steps from shadow with the same eyes and the same two tails, as if certainty itself split in half.

---

## Living Rite Rules (self-contained)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Bright beat action | Safe window for crack/swap actions |
| Off-pulse strike | Ritual Hunger -1 and nearest Bound Voice harmed |
| Three on-pulse anchor successes | Rite paused; Ritual Hunger -2 |
| Free without swap | Bound voice freed, but Ritual Hunger -1 and next Fear escalates |
| Swap vessel | Bound voice freed with no hunger surge |
| Kill Tavore while all three bound | Ritual surge; Ritual Hunger -2 and one Bound Voice takes severe harm |

Counter-rite:
- Fianna may invoke *two cuttings for one taken* once (Presence 14): success gives Ritual Hunger -1 and one ally clears 1 Stress.

---

## Mechanical Teaching

Use [[Shaela Sagebloom]] to reinforce timing:

> [!QUOTE]
> "Not on the inhale. On the flare. Wait, then cut."

Allow Gnirth or Fern to identify beat spacing with Instinct/Knowledge 13.

---

## Environmental Challenges

1. **Pulse Backlash:** If a spellcast happens off-beat, caster marks 1 Stress.
2. **Anchor Snap:** Agility 14 to avoid lash when an anchor cracks.
3. **Temple Steps Flood:** Strength 13 to hold footing at Very Close.

---

## Fear Spends

- Re-conjure Doppelhound duplicate in the worst possible angle.
- Fowlbear Dread Honk scatters PCs off beat-window positions or away from Tavore.
- Cult Initiate Group Attack on a PC mid-crack/swap.
- Tavore issues a command surge: one ally takes immediate move or attack.
- Greenfire surge pushes all creatures one range away from center anchor.
- Tick Ritual Hunger on high-stakes failure with Fear at anchors.

---

## Lane Win Conditions

Any two:
- Doppelhound removed or neutralized.
- Fowlbear removed, driven off, or pulled away from Tavore's flank.
- One anchor safely cracked/swapped.
- Tavore hesitates or is displaced from command position.
- Bound Voices remain alive and responsive.

Then move to [[Session 20 Scene 5 - Reverse the Door]].
