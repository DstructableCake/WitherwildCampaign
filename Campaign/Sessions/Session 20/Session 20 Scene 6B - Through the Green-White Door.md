# Scene 6B: Through the Green-White Door

*Scene Type:* Spirit Threshold | Short Extension  
*Checkpoint:* End-of-session fork B

---

## Goal

Give a short, decisive first look into the spirit-side conflict without starting a full second raid.

---

## Entry Conditions

Run only if:
- table has energy,
- at least 20 minutes remain,
- players choose to push through immediately.

---

## Read-Aloud

> [!QUOTE]
> The tear does not open into distance. It opens into a second version of the same grove, lit from beneath by living veins of pale fire.  
> Every tree stands where it should, but each one bears two shadows.  
> In the roots ahead, three chained echoes kneel where the bound voices stood, and the beat is now a heartbeat under your feet.

---

## Short Spirit Challenges (pick 2)

1. **Echo Chain:** Presence or Knowledge 14 to identify which chain is real and which is bait.
2. **Mirror Predator:** Doppelhound-like echo strike from impossible angles; Instinct 14 to read truth.
3. **Drowning Light:** Strength or Agility 13 to move through syrup-thick green haze.

On any failed check with Fear, mark one party Stress and foreshadow a spirit adversary for Session 22.

---

## One Meaningful Choice

Offer a clear single decision:

- **Cut:** break one echo chain now, risking imbalance.
- **Hold:** stabilize rhythm and withdraw with intel.
- **Bargain:** address the unseen presence behind the pulse.

Whichever they choose defines Session 22 opening state.

---

## End Frame

Close on a revelation:

- A voice in Nikta's cadence says one PC's true name.
- Tavore's silhouette appears on the far side of mirrored roots.
- Gnirth sees floodwater running uphill through the spirit grove.

Then end session.
