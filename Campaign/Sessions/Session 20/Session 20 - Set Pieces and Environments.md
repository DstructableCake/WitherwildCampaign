# Session 20 - Set Pieces and Environments

This reference keeps environmental pressure and Fear usage intentional across all lanes.

---

## Shared Environment Tone

- Storm rain limits visibility and footing.
- Living roots react to Fear and ritual pulses.
- Green-white fire surges are tied to the ritual beat.

---

## Lane A Set Piece - Rooted Escape Route

Use during [[Session 20 Scene 2 - Pens to Feather Edge]]:

### Environment Moves

- **Mud Shear:** Target at Close or farther must pass Agility 14 or lose one range of movement.
- **Root Hook:** Instinct 13 or become temporarily Restrained at route chokepoint.
- **Burning Cart Burst:** Close blast zone deals moderate physical damage on failed reaction.

### Fear Spends

- Split protectors from civilians by one range.
- Force escort decision: continue movement or rescue a fallen hostage.
- Trigger Pangolati Blade Fling from hard cover.

---

## Lane B Set Piece - Living Rite Anchors

Use during [[Session 20 Scene 3 - Temple Under Living Rite]] and [[Session 20 Scene 5 - Reverse the Door]]:

### Environment Moves

- **Pulse Backlash:** Off-beat ritual action causes stress pulse to acting PC.
- **Anchor Lash:** Failed crack/swap attempt creates immediate whip recoil.
- **Flooded Steps:** Reposition checks become harder until next bright beat.

### Fear Spends

- Tick Ritual Hunger on failure with Fear at anchor actions.
- Force duplicate pressure: Doppelhound appears where confidence is highest.
- Fowlbear Dread Honk drives PCs off Tavore's flank or out of a bright-beat window.
- Cult Initiate Group Attack piles onto whoever is mid-swap at an anchor.
- Escalate to Greenfire Surge pushing creatures one range.

---

## Lane C Set Piece - Elder Platform Collapse

Use during [[Session 20 Scene 4 - Elder Platforms and Antlers]]:

### Environment Moves

- **Bridge Snap:** On heavy movement, bridge fails after one warning beat.
- **Root Eruption:** Thorny growth creates temporary hazard zone.
- **Sway Line:** Ranged actions from high platform suffer disadvantage unless stabilized.

### Fear Spends

- Leshen Root Binding interrupts a rescue reach.
- Leshen roots pin a freed warrior again unless protected.
- Shift one safe route into contested terrain.

---

## Cross-Lane Fear Budget

Use this budget to avoid over-spiking one front:

- **Early fight (first 25%):** 1-2 Fear per lane total.
- **Mid escalation (25-75%):** 2-4 Fear per lane total.
- **Convergence (final 25%):** Save at least 3 Fear for ritual/tear drama.

---

## Clock-Driven Hazards

- **Ritual Hunger at 3:** All pulse-based actions gain visible instability.
- **Ritual Hunger at 1:** Reclaim pressure manifests (whispers, chain recoil, light fracture).
- **Grove Collapse at 4:** Non-temple routes become harder to cross safely.
- **Grove Collapse at 1:** Retreat paths start failing in sequence.

---

## Environment Challenge Prompts (for player creativity)

- "What do you sacrifice to gain safe footing here?"
- "What part of this grove still remembers you?"
- "What do you break to save someone else right now?"
- "What fear does this pulse put in your body?"
