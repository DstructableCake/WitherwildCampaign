# Scene 2: Pens to Feather Edge

*Scene Type:* Escort Combat | Route Pressure  
*Checkpoint:* Lane A

---

## Goal

Move prisoners and children to safer ground while under beast interception.

---

## Adversary Package

- Primary: [[Dire Pangolati]]
- Soft support: one [[Bladed Guard]] (Circle warden)
- If a second PC rotates in: [[Conscript]] ×2

Do not open with Pangolati + Hunter + Guard. Armor Eater already makes this lane sharp for one protector.

---

## Battlefield Notes

- Narrow mud paths between split-root berms.
- Two hard-cover carts, one already burning.
- Rain and roots make footing unstable; failed movement can cost position.

---

## Running the Lane

- Advance **Cage Run** once per hostage group crossing.
- On failed crossing roll with Fear, either:
  - Cage Run stalls for one exchange, or
  - a hostage group becomes exposed to Blade Fling.

Use Armor Eater to create hard armor decisions without instantly downing PCs.

---

## Environmental Challenges

Use one per round max:

1. **Root Snag:** Agility 14 to keep movement pace; fail -> move one range less.
2. **Burning Cart Drop:** Instinct 13 to spot collapse; fail -> take minor physical damage and lose position.
3. **Panic Surge:** Presence 13 to steady civilians; fail -> Cage Run does not tick this exchange.

---

## Fear Spends

- Pangolati Blade Spin while PCs cluster around hostages.
- Pangolati Blade Fling at rear-guard instead of front-liner.
- Guard pushes civilians back toward danger.

---

## Character Spotlights

- **Fianna:** choose between wildform aggression and direct protection role.
- **Snicket (if she rotates in):** route cutting and hostage motion tricks.
- **Fionnula:** gives tactical local route advice if protected.

---

## Lane Win Conditions

Any two:
- Children reach Feather Edge.
- Fionnula reaches cover alive.
- Pangolati driven off / defeated.
- Recapture prevented for one full round after last crossing.

When lane is won, free one PC to rotate to convergence early.
