# Scene 4: Elder Platforms and Antlers

*Scene Type:* Vertical Combat | Rescue | Moral Branch  
*Checkpoint:* Lane C

---

## Goal

Break the hold on pinned warriors and secure elder lanes while surviving Leshen control pressure.

---

## Adversary Package

- Primary boss: [[Leshen Warden]]
- Minions: [[Tangle Bramble]] ×2
- Kill-branch escalate: fuse ignored Brambles into a [[Tangle Bramble Swarm]], or refresh two more Brambles

---

## Read-Aloud Cue

> [!QUOTE]
> The antlers rise before the body does, crowned in rain and old bark. Root-bands tighten around pinned warriors as the platform groans under the beast's waking weight.  
> Below the bridges, two blood-drinking brambles tumble and drink the rain — no second hunter, only the Leshen and whatever the roots still hold.

---

## Battlefield Notes

- Three connected platforms with rope bridges.
- One bridge is half-severed and fails on second heavy crossing.
- Root growth creates shifting difficult terrain each round.

---

## Mercy or Kill Branch Triggers

### Mercy Branch

Trigger if PCs:
- call for nonlethal intent,
- target root brands,
- attempt communication tied to grove duty.

Run Leshen feature **Sever the Brand** and free pinned warriors as successes land.

Result:
- Leshen flees or kneels.
- Grove Collapse improves by 1.

### Kill Branch

Trigger if PCs commit to direct lethal pressure.

Result:
- Leshen must be fully cleared.
- No Grove Collapse reduction.

Both outcomes count as lane completion.

---

## Environmental Challenges

1. **Bridge Shear:** Agility 14 while crossing under impact or fall to lower root shelf.
2. **Root Clamp:** Instinct 14 to avoid temporary Restrained on failed reposition.
3. **Swaying Perch:** Finesse 13 for ranged attack stability on elevated perches.

---

## Fear Spends

- Leshen uses Root Binding when a PC tries to reach pinned warriors.
- Re-pin a just-freed warrior unless someone stays to protect them.
- Spotlight both Brambles as a Group Attack on a PC holding a bridge.
- Platform collapse advance: one safe lane becomes hazardous.

---

## Character Spotlights

- **Fern:** corruption language and mercy-path leadership.
- **Snicket:** route and extraction control on unstable vertical map.
- **Sabriel/Gnirth (if rotating):** hard commitment call between speed and rescue.

---

## Lane Win Conditions

Any two:
- Elders/warriors freed from active restraint.
- Leshen resolved (mercy or kill).
- Platform route to temple remains passable.

Then move to [[Session 20 Scene 5 - Reverse the Door]].
