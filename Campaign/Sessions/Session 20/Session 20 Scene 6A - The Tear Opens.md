# Scene 6A: The Tear Opens

*Scene Type:* Catastrophic Climax | Cliffhanger End  
*Checkpoint:* End-of-session fork A

---

## Goal

Deliver a hard emotional and visual cliffhanger that locks in Session 22 momentum.

---

## Read-Aloud

> [!QUOTE]
> The last bright beat comes wrong. Not a pulse - a wrench.  
> Green-white fire stretches like thread pulled through torn bark. The roots of the Great Tree scream against stone.  
> Someone laughs once through the storm, distant and close at the same time. Tavore's mask jerks skyward as if hooked from inside.  
> The air peels open.
