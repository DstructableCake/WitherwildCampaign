# Session 20 Prep: Signal, Siege, and the Living Door

**Theme:** Rescue under collapsing ritual pressure.  
**Setting:** Whitefeather Circle / Nikta's Grove during the storm raid.

**TL;DR:** Start at the attack signal and Fionnula's warning, run three combat lanes with shared clocks, reconverge at the Living Rite for the reverse window, then choose either cliffhanger tear ending or brief spirit-side push based on table energy.

---

## Title Card Quote

> [!QUOTE] [[Fionnula]]
> "Do not cut the chain mid-breath. Wait for the bright beat, or the grove tears with us."

## Fear Economy (Spend Menu)

- Spotlight Relentless enemies (Leshen, Fowlbear cadence, Tavore surge).
- Re-conjure Doppelhound duplicate at a bad angle for PCs.
- Trigger environmental pressure:
  - Temple greenfire pulse backlash
  - Root lash from floor seams
  - Storm gust that shifts range bands or footing
- Tick **Ritual Hunger** when PCs push wrong-time ritual actions.
- Escalate **Grove Collapse** on high-chaos misses with Fear.

## Lane Assignment and Adversary Plan

### Lane A: Pens and Escape Route

- Focus PCs: **Fianna** first; others can rotate in by fiction.
- Adversary package: [[Dire Pangolati]] + one [[Bladed Guard]] (Circle warden). If a second PC joins, add [[Conscript]] ×2.
- Tactical feel: armored predator punishes armor use and over-committed defense; escort clocks do the rest.
- Win conditions:
  - Move children and freed prisoners toward Feather Edge
  - Keep Fionnula alive
  - Prevent recapture while Cage Run advances

### Lane B: Temple Rite Diversion

- Focus PCs: **Sabriel** and **Gnirth**
- Adversary package: [[Fowlbear]] guarding [[The Masked Commander|Tavore]], [[Doppelhound]], [[Cult Initiate]] ×2; bring Tavore's Leader sheet (3 BP) when pressed
- Tactical feel: bruiser bodyguard + positional deception; initiates clog anchors; Tavore commands rather than out-damaging the Fowlbear
- Win conditions:
  - Hold pressure without feeding Ritual Hunger
  - Create windows for swap/crack actions
  - Keep Bound Voices viable
  - Displace or wear down the Fowlbear enough to reach Tavore

### Lane C: Elders and Platform Holds

- Focus PCs: **Fern** and **Snicket**
- Adversary package: [[Leshen Warden]] (buffed) + [[Tangle Bramble]] ×2
- Tactical feel: one Solo boss on a vertical map; brambles create rescue friction, not a dual-elite.
- Win conditions:
  - Free or secure elders/warriors
  - Resolve Leshen through mercy or kill branch
  - Keep path to convergence open

---

## Branch Card: Leshen Outcome (Play Either)

### Mercy Branch

- Trigger if PCs attempt freeing pinned warriors, ritual rhythm interaction, or nonlethal intent.
- Use **Sever the Brand** from Leshen stat block.
- Rewards:
  - +1 Hope to group
  - Reduce Grove Collapse by 1
  - Surviving warriors can reinforce temple convergence

### Kill Branch

- Trigger if PCs commit to direct elimination.
- Leshen fights to full clear; keep or refresh [[Tangle Bramble]] ×2 for action economy.
- Consequences:
  - Caitlin approves immediate decisiveness
  - Grove grief tone rises
  - No collapse reduction

Both branches count as a lane victory and move to convergence.

## Reverse-Rite Rules (Self-Contained)

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Bright beat action | Safe window for crack/swap actions |
| Off-pulse strike | Ritual Hunger -1 and nearest Bound Voice takes harm |
| Three on-pulse anchor successes | Rite paused; Ritual Hunger -2 |
| Free without swap | Bound voice freed, but Ritual Hunger -1 and next Fear spend escalates |
| Swap vessel | Bound voice freed with no hunger surge |
| Kill Tavore while all three still bound | Immediate surge, Ritual Hunger -2, one Bound Voice takes serious harm |

Counter-rite option:
- Fianna may invoke *two cuttings for one taken* once (Presence 14). On success: Ritual Hunger -1 and one ally clears 1 Stress.

## End Fork Decision Rule

Choose at table with no pause:

- If under 30 minutes, party spent, or dramatic momentum favors shock ending -> run **Scene 6A** (cliffhanger).
- If table energy is high and players push through immediately -> run **Scene 6B** (brief spirit-side opening).
