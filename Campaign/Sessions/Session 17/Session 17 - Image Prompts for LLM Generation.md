# Session 17 - Image Prompts for LLM Generation

Use these prompts in an external image-generation tool.  
Each prompt is **fully self-contained** — the generator has no campaign context. Copy one prompt block in full.

**Recommended output:** 16:9 landscape for scenes; 4:5 or 3:4 for portraits.  
**Consistency tip:** Reuse the same approved character seed across prompts for each recurring PC or NPC.

---

## Global Style (append to every prompt)

`Highly detailed oil painting, dark fantasy realism, rich impasto brush texture, layered glazing, visible brushstrokes, dramatic natural lighting, cinematic depth of field, atmospheric perspective, intricate material detail, weathered surfaces, damp forest atmosphere, restrained color palette with luminous accents, no text, no letters, no watermark, no modern objects.`

## 1) Beat 1 — Dawn on the Sweet Road

**Shot:** Wide establishing landscape, eye-level to slight low angle, 24mm cinematic feel, party as mid-ground subjects on a curving trail, deep forest recession into background, rule-of-thirds placement with trail leading from lower left toward luminous center.

**Time & light:** Early Sunnar dawn — sky holds an unnatural painted gradient (amber to pale green) as if the day is stuck; warm golden side-light through pollen haze, long soft shadows, dew glitter on every surface.

**Environment:** Overgrown Fanewick forest road. Pollen visible as golden dust in sunbeams. Sunrise vines actively climbing tree trunks. Sap gleams on bark like wet lacquer. A **bark-ridged deer** watches from brush without fear. Trail shows **conflicting tracks**: pilgrim lichen marks, game paths, and **older heavy cage-wheel ruts** cutting deeper than recent prints. Optional: discarded coil of sun-bleached bridge rope half-buried in roots; faint thread of smoke on the wind from behind the party.

**Theme:** Abundance as warning — the world is beautiful and wrong.

**Party actions (specific):**
- **Fern** walks slightly apart, one hand raised toward a split in the trail, reading growth patterns; wings folded, expression wary.
- **Fianna** pauses beside an **owl-scrape glyph** carved in bark, fingertips near the mark, head tilted as if listening.
- **Sabriel** kneels briefly at overlapping prints — **Haven military heel marks** crossed by **Wickling reed-knot** trail markers — studying them with soldier's focus.
- **Snicket** crouches at rut depth, measuring wheel spacing with two fingers, hood half up, purple hair catching light.
- **Gnirth** stands at a stream edge in background, staring at water that looks **unnaturally clear**, cupped hand not yet touching surface, posture tense.

**Oil painting emphasis:** Thick pollen in air, wet bark highlights, contrasting warm light and cool shadow in undergrowth.

## 2) Beat 2 — The Green Cathedral of Nikta's Lower Eye

**Shot:** Grand interior-environment vista, low angle looking upward through living nave, party small-to-medium in lower third, cathedral architecture dominates frame — trees as rib-vaults, vines as tracery, roots as columns.

**Time & light:** Mid-morning; god-rays through leaf canopy like stained glass; motes of spore and pollen; patches of rot-brown at vine tips contrasting emerald growth.

**Environment:** A **sacred forest cathedral built entirely of nature** — no stone. Generations of **Lantern Sower** caretakers trained living arches here for **Nikta, the Great Owl** (divinity of sowing and reaping cycles). **Failed balance** now shows as overflow: mega-ferns, fox-length songbirds at eye level, oversized hare with velvet antlers in burrow, **owl-face carved in living bark with one eye overgrown shut**. **Root altar** with five weathered offering hollows (five-gift rite). **Reed-knots** at shoulder height half-swallowed by new vines. **Discarded iron-wrap bundle** outside old sacred boundary (no-iron custom). Faded caretaker tally stick motif in roots.

**Theme:** Wonder becoming ecological panic — devotion without reaping.

**Party actions (specific):**
- **Fianna** kneels before the **antlered hare**, one hand extended but not grabbing — mercy versus pragmatism — face soft with conflict.
- **Fern** examines a **corrupted bloom** that almost looks healing, holding bone charm near it, dark eye-stains stark in side-light, wings slightly unfurled in agitation.
- **Gnirth** at a still black-veined pool edge, both hands trembling toward water, fighting renewal urge, reflection distorted in pool.
- **Snicket** on a **parallel elevated game trail** above reeds (or root shelf), scouting route, body low, one hand on mossy limb.
- **Sabriel** shields **Fianna** or a smaller party member as a **vine-laden branch creaks downward** — sword still sheathed, free arm raised against "gentle" collapsing growth.

**Optional background detail:** Distant horn call with no answering herd; Rohkin-sign chew marks on sapling.

**Oil painting emphasis:** Sap gloss, spore haze, sacred carvings in bark texture, scale contrast between tiny humans and cathedral trees.

## 4) Beat 3 — Crossing Node (Ford Social Scene)

**Environment:** Practical **crossing node** — stiff sap-sticky rope bridge OR fast ankle-deep ford. **Cancelled smuggler chalk** on stump. **Stick-map** with X over high-tree route. Packed bundles of displaced travelers. Clear water, brittle social tension.

**NPCs (describe fully in frame):**

**Theme:** Fanewick as people, not monsters — trust is currency.

## Usage Tips

- Paste **Global Style** at the end of every prompt if your tool has a short character limit, prioritize subject/environment first, style last.
- For **party scenes**, generate at 16:9; crop to character focus if needed.
- If outputs are too clean: add `grime, worn leather, mud spatters, frayed cloth, blood-speck, ash smear, humid haze`.
- If outputs are too dark: add `luminous pollen, warm god-rays, firelight accents, bioluminescent spore glow`.
- Lock character identity by reusing one seed image per PC across prompts 1–14.
