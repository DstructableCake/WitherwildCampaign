# Daggerheart – Session 10 Notes

**Title:** _Ghost of the Past_  
**Theme:** Old allies surface in the dark. Trust thins. Rescue and ruin share the same rope.  
**Setting:** From [[Elowen]]'s hidden cottage to the scholars' tree and the twisted prison beneath [[Alula]], the party splits to find Theodore and map the Fanewraith's web before Sunnar's dawn.

---

## Elowen's counsel

[[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]] and [[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]] spoke with [[Elowen]] in her hut before they left. The air still held the warmth of stew. The place felt untouched by the worst of the Witherwild.

She named council members she had known in better times. [[Elder Corin]], [[Elder Mirra]], and [[Elder Kaelen]] might still answer the right ask. Of [[Fanewraith|Elara Voss]], the woman the world called the Fanewraith, she offered only a sliver of history. Elara had reached for the faint divinities young, and that hunger had never left. It had twisted.

Executions, she said, needed two signatures. One from the council of elders. One from the guard corps. Alter the document, and [[Theodore Underbrush|Theodore]] might yet escape the noose. Where Elara was now, Elowen could not say. She came and went like a shadow. Fern and Sabriel took what they needed and climbed back toward the world above.

---

## The market in the treetops

In Alula's canopy, [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]], [[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]], and [[Gnirth]] made for the market. A finer platform caught the light high above. Below, the main space opened like an amphitheatre, the hangman's noose at its focus. Eight feet high. Ringed with guards. Stalls crowded the walkways. Wanted posters were everywhere. Charcoal sketches of a halfling, an orc, and others, smeared with graffiti that named them traitors, liars, thieves.

Fianna drew Snicket aside. The halfling on the poster was her father. They would learn what he was accused of.

The market held Ironside Forge, the Enchanted Emporium, Healer's Hearth, the Traveller's Trove, and more. In the Emporium a small elven keeper tended the wares. An arcane prism, rubbed, sharpened nearby spellcasting until the next long rest. A Valor Stone raised the holder's damage threshold until rest. An improved wand. The prism went for a handful of gold. Snicket talked it down to three. She asked after the halfling's bounty. If the posters already named him captured, the keeper said, he would already be in their hands.

At the forge a strong dwarf woman with a surprisingly high voice showed improved weapons and a misty orb of a gem. Attune it to a blade, she said, and on a one or a two the bearer could reroll the weapon attack. At the Traveller's Trove a young faun-like keeper sold travelling gear, old gloves, keys, lockets. A Gecko Glove for three gold let the wearer cling to surfaces. They skipped Healer's Hearth.

They returned to the Emporium and bought the prism at three gold. Then a civilian tripped over Gnirth, invisible and hiding. The noise drew the guards. The three slipped away as the patrol turned their way.

---

## Religion and rendezvous

Elsewhere Sabriel and Fern chose the tavern for the reunion. The Fungril network was not safe. On the walk they spoke of religion and where Fanewick's powers rose from. Fern spoke of her own faith. Sabriel pressed further. Learn where Elara and Elowen had come from. Learn which faint divinity Elara served. That might show when to strike, or how to weaken her. He went further still. Perhaps they could strike at Elara's god.

Fern told him to sleep. Then she reached for Gnirth on the network and sent a simple signal. A two. An L. Gnirth answered: *return to sender*. She sent back: *Rendezvous*.

---

## The noose and the whisper

Snicket, Fianna, and Gnirth made for the inn. At the hangman's rope they counted three guards close and two watching from farther off. Gnirth felt a tingle and studied the liquid on the rope. Thick. Not water. Not common oil. It reminded him of marsh tar.

Around them people whispered about Fianna's father. He had sold secrets to Haven, they said.

---

## Reunion and the split

At the inn they sorted a brief muddle over Fern's coded description, then laid out what they knew. The plan settled. Sabriel and Gnirth would go to the council and the scholars. Snicket, Fern, and Fianna would go to the dungeons.

---

## Into the earth

Snicket, Fern, and Fianna followed [[Elowen]]'s mole companion, Demetrius, down the path he had dug. They spoke as they went. When the way was clear he wished them luck and vanished into the soil.

---

## The twisted tree

They came up in the shadow of the corrupted tree. Eighty feet and more of tainted growth. Thorns as long as a forearm. Above, the village's bustle thinned into night. No guards waited below.

Fern flew to the windows. The bars were tainted. She could not make out anyone inside. A single entrance led into the prison from above, heavily guarded. Fianna took hummingbird form and slipped through the cells. In one she found a young boy, still and thin. Fern offered a little cake. His eyes opened. He screamed. She pulled back and hid.

Fianna moved room to room until she found what she had come for. A man chained to the wall. A bundle of fur in the corner. She flew to him and hummed the song they had once shared.

"Dad," she said. "It's me. Fianna."

Light returned to his eyes.

She told him everything. Theodore said he had left in anger, hunting revenge, and had met [[Kreil Durn|Kreil]], who spoke of the Fanewraith. He had found [[Brindle]] in the woods already turned, tearing into his mate. He had put Brindle down. But he had a secret.

"Let me introduce you to Brindle's daughter, Faith."

---

## The scholars' tree

Sabriel and Gnirth rode the lift they had learned of earlier and walked to the scholars' desk with confidence. The man there was [[Elder Corin]]. Charismatic. Academic. Robes and bearing of someone who belonged among the archives. Suspicion sat on him from the first word. Sabriel's subtlety landed nowhere. Sabriel wrote Elowen's name on a scrap, then crumpled it. Corin said, "Come with me." They followed.

He led them into a room of circular bookcases and ladders, stairs spiralling up the outside of the trunk. Near the top they entered a chamber. The door sealed behind them with a hum of magic. Nothing in. Nothing out.

Sabriel told him Kreil had put them onto Elowen, and Elowen had sent them to Corin. They meant to stop the Fanewraith, Elara Voss. He gave what they knew of Bolgin, and that Bolgin lived still on the ethereal plane. He asked how to deal with Bolgin. Corin spoke of flowers that grew only in Fanewick. One person on the council could speak to Bolgin and the ethereal plane. He did not like discussing it. He would send for her anyway.

Sabriel asked about the tainted tree. Corin said it had come from the corruption and had never been part of Alula. It arrived as a treant after Bolgin was killed. Reports spoke of an explosion around the time of Bolgin's death. Gnirth asked where. Corin said the archive held maps, and he might know someone who had been involved. Of Fern's grove he claimed ignorance. Of Theodore he claimed only this. Theo was a traitor. Pressed on what Theodore had done, Corin said he had given secrets to Haven.

"I'm from Haven," Sabriel said. "You're talking to me. Does that not make you a traitor?"

Corin's answer was cold.

"No. It makes you a fly caught in a web."

A bang at the door. As it opened, Sabriel drove his blade into Corin.

---

## A voice from the past

Sabriel drew the sword free. The door shut behind the figure who had struck it. A knight. She stopped. A woman's voice cut the silence.

"Sabriel. You haven't changed one bit."

The session ended there. Theodore found, and a secret waiting in a bundle of fur. Sabriel and Gnirth sealed in a chamber high above Alula, facing a voice from the past.
