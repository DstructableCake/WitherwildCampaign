# Daggerheart – Session 9 Notes

**Title:** _Eyes in the Canopy_  
**Theme:** Hidden truths. Divided paths. Family bonds under pressure. In the treetops every branch has eyes, and trust is spent carefully.  
**Setting:** [[Alula]], three layers above the forest floor. The party seeks [[Theodore Underbrush|Theodore]] and the Fanewraith's hold on the city, watched at every turn, with Sunnar's dawn closing in.

---

## Arrival at the gates

The path climbed. The river's hush faded. Treetops loomed. Find Theodore. Find the Fanewraith's hideout. First, get inside.

Guards watched every traveler. The party split the approach.

[[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]], [[Gnirth]], and [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]], the last shaped as a small cat-bat-mouse hybrid, came as merchants. Delivery for Mr. Tilde. Legitimate business. The guard's skepticism held, then gave. A nod. Passage.

[[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]] and [[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]] came as refugees. A narrowed look. A gold coin changed hands. The gate opened. The guard's gaze followed them, intense and unblinking. Noted. Watched.

Snicket set the serpent coin atop her package, a quiet signal for later checkpoints.

---

## Gnirth's disturbing vision

Deeper in Alula's lower levels, Gnirth felt the Fungril network through roots and soil. Something felt wrong. He reached out through the trees for guidance or word of the path ahead.

The response shocked him.

Vision went dark. Absolute black. Then eyes. Dozens. Hundreds. Piercing the dark, fixed on him. Everywhere. The sense of being observed from every angle, fully exposed.

He pulled back. The network severed under those watching eyes. Paranoia settled in. They were not alone. Something monitored them through the very net he trusted. The trees themselves had become eyes.

---

## Through the thorned gate

Further in, a thorned gate. Another checkpoint. Snicket offered a bribe. The guard insisted on searching her bag.

On top of everything, the serpent coin.

Their eyes met. Still air. Then a subtle nod. Through without further question. The coin had opened the door. Easy passage was also a warning. Fanewraith ground. Symbols meant privilege and danger both.

Descending a walkway past the refugee checkpoint, they saw something on the forest floor even from that height. Dark pulsing energy, writhing. Corruption ran deep here too. The Witherwild's touch reached even this sanctuary in the branches.

---

## The Sump Lantern Tavern

Fern and Sabriel chose a tavern for information. Gossip. Deals. Whispered secrets. The Sump Lantern stood marked by lanterns that threw shifting shadows across the walkways.

Before they went in, Fianna warned Gnirth of the destination through their link. His reply was cautious. Be careful. They did not know if the connection was compromised, or if the watching eyes could hear as well as see.

Inside, Fern and Sabriel took a corner table with a clear view of the room. Shady deals in low voices. Hooded figures slumped at tables. A distant card game. Patrons of many races and colors, keeping to themselves unless they had company. Trust was scarce.

One creature in the corner had been studying them with quiet judgment. Sabriel slid a chair out from their table and looked away. Invitation without words that might draw attention.

---

## Silent Mara's offer

After a moment the creature approached. A young female Ribbet, face partly hidden, skin unmistakable. She bounced into the offered chair and introduced herself as Mara.

Fern gave the name Celeste. Sabriel, Tarn. False names that felt necessary. Mara knew. Common here. In Alula, truth was often a liability.

Straight to business. Looking for work? She had jobs. Deadly if caught. For those who needed coin or favor and would take the risk.

Sabriel bartered for information. Stories of their journey. Serpents Hope. Threats faced. In return Mara explained Alula's transport. Ziplines and pulleys between levels, run by people who knew the city's secrets. The Astronomer's Tower had a pulley from the base of the tree. Useful, maybe.

He pressed about Fianna's father, light on the question, mentioning the struggle at the gate, wondering if Theodore was the only one taken. Mara knew little. Answers vague.

When he asked how to find the Fanewraith her expression shifted. She did not know. Even if she did, they should not seek her. Danger followed those questions.

She rose, leaving a small card with her insignia. A froggy thumbprint pointing toward the refugee camp. A token for later.

Fianna, still linked, passed word of the ziplines, pulleys, and Astronomer's Tower so the others had it too.

---

## Mr. Tilde's delivery

Snicket, Gnirth, and Fianna pushed into Shadow Root, winding walkways and rope bridges in the lower city. Destination: Mr. Tilde's shop. A delivery long overdue.

The shop sat among the roots, marked by hanging charms and dried herbs. Inside, Mr. Tilde waited. Galapa. Abrupt. Sarcasm that seeped through every line. Snicket met it with polite patience and a rant about the state of society that seemed to amuse him despite himself.

Fianna, still transformed, scurried the corners. A sensation of being watched crept over her. A shadow at the edge of vision, plain when she was not looking straight at it. Even here, not alone.

Snicket handed over the package. Mr. Tilde nodded and vanished into the back. He returned with something wrapped in silken cloth.

A dagger. Tier 2. Beautifully made. D8+4 damage, Reliable. He had been asked to pass it to whoever delivered the package. Origins unknown. Gift from a mutual party.

Snicket asked about the extra guards. Refugees flooding in as trouble spread across Fanewick, he said. Trade routes broken. Bandits and monsters on the rise. The city preparing for war whether it wanted to or not.

One more request. Would she complete a delivery for him somewhere else in Haven? Discretion utmost. Do not lose it.

She agreed, then asked after Fianna's father. Where he might be held. Mr. Tilde's answer was cold. Every sunrise, those guilty of treason found themselves on the end of a rope.

Only a couple of days until Sunnar. Not much time to find Theodore, face the Fanewraith, and plan an escape.

Gnirth, wary of watching eyes, tried to pass Mr. Tilde's news to Fern at the tavern. Vague. Careful. Every word might be overheard.

---

## The cursed name

At the tavern Sabriel studied Silent Mara's card, the froggy thumbprint a rough map toward the refugee camp. As she prepared to leave he called her back. Low voice. He asked about [[Elowen]], the woman from dawn.

Mara hushed him at once. Eyes scanning the room. That name was cursed here. Be careful who heard it. Some names carried death in Alula.

---

## The paid room

A Simiah bartender approached. Their rooms were ready. Paid for by a "friend."

Fern and Sabriel exchanged looks. Who had paid? Who knew they were here? Mara's goodwill, or someone watching since they entered the city?

They followed his directions into the tree. A trunk grand enough to hollow for spiral stairs and rooms carved in living wood. Down and down until they were surely underground.

Down, left, down further, room on the left. Roots becoming walls and floors. Air cooler with each step.

At the door they paused. No sound inside. Unlocked. It opened freely.

A modest room. Wardrobe. Desk and chairs grown from the tree. A simple bed. On the bed, watching with dark intelligent eyes, a mole.

Demetrius.

Elowen's companion from dawn. Waiting. They entered and shut the door.

---

## The hidden passage

Behind the wardrobe, a hole in the corner dug by patient claws. Demetrius moved to it, looked back, beckoned. Follow.

They crawled after him. Narrow. Dark. Long. Hundreds of meters from the village, through earth and root, led by a creature who knew exactly where he was going.

---

## Fianna's disturbing communion

Back in Alula, Fianna, Snicket, and Gnirth left Mr. Tilde's. They still needed a way past the guards to the root cellar and the next delivery. First Fianna touched a nearby tree and asked where her father was.

Hunger answered. Deep. Gnawing. From the tree itself. The trees were watching. Waiting. Seeking the nutrients that would come at sunrise. Waiting to feed.

"Feed. Feed. Feed. Feed."

The chant taxed her thoughts and stressed her. Alula's trees were not passive. They waited for the dawn hangings, ready to take the bodies. A truth she wished she had not learned.

---

## Evading the guards

Leaving the shop they saw more guards. More visible. More watchful. On instinct Snicket dragged Gnirth back inside.

She cast invisibility on him, then disguised herself as an orc. Past the guards toward the cellar and the package.

Necessary. And a reminder how dangerous the place had become.

---

## The root cellar

Third tree on the left. Thick branches hosting platforms out from the trunk. Architectural quarter. Builders. Engineers. Constructors. Little wildlife. An eeriness that seemed to keep animals away.

Access tunnels down. Midway, a crane-like construct lifted a platform of goods toward the upper levels, mechanisms creaking. At the bottom, a desk and tunnels heading deep underground.

A bespectacled elf at the ledger, quill moving hard. Keeper of the cellar and "things." Someone who had seen much and said little.

They handed over the package. It vanished from sight at once. Brief. Efficient. He wanted them gone.

---

## The trap

While Snicket spoke with the keeper, Fianna in creature form slipped down the tunnels. Crates. Boxes. The detritus of a city's hidden infrastructure.

Footprints every which way. One set different. Toward a wall, then gone, as if someone had walked through solid wood.

She crept closer. A snap.

A cage closed around her small form. The sound carried. The elf and Snicket could not ignore it.

Annoyance on the keeper's face more than surprise, as if this happened often. Fianna talked him into releasing the cat-bat creature. They left before more trouble, back up toward the canopies. The vanishing footprints unanswered for now.

---

## Elowen's truth

Underground, Sabriel and Fern followed Demetrius farther into the earth. Hundreds of meters of crawl past the village's edge.

Fresh air at last. The tunnel opened into forest.

Untouched by Witherwild taint. Healthy bush and undergrowth. A home built into tree roots, half-hidden by the ground. Hearty stew on the air. Door ajar. Warm glow within.

Fern checked for traps. None. She tried Gnirth. Something blocked her. Cut off from the outside. Fungril network silenced.

They entered.

Elowen.

The woman from dawn. Meal shared. Kindness offered. Waiting. Her home a sanctuary hidden from the corruption consuming Alula.

She confirmed it. Alula was dangerous, and the others were in it now. She had truths they needed.

---

## The story of Elara Voss

Elowen spoke from memory and loss. The Fanewraith had once been Elara, her niece, in her thirties. The sweet girl was gone.

Years ago, an assault on their lands. Elara was already a skilled hunter. After the atrocities she saw, something broke. Havenites stood against the natural order. They had caused the war. They were the enemy.

She lost everything and would not be stopped. She took the mantle of the Fanewraith. Healer to shadow and death.

Over months she built a name as hunter, assassin, and link between the natural world and the spirit. Somehow she learned to speak with spirits. High command in Haven took notice. They wanted her for a suicide mission. Steal the Reaping Eye of Nikta. Return it to Haven. Kick off the everlasting bloom.

Through Fanewick connections she struck Nikta at her weakest, stole the eye, and vanished. Nikta fled to the Astral Realm and left the mortal world.

Elara gained control of Alula by bribery, shadow work, and leverage. Anyone tied to her past was cut down. Only Elowen remained. She had escaped some time ago. Anyone using that name in Alula would be marked and would not see the next sunrise.

Elowen did not know who in the village was loyal to the Fanewraith now. Careful who they spoke to. "In the canopy, every branch has eyes. Trust the ones that do not look back."

---

## The twisted vine tree

It might already be too late for Theodore, she said. Much hidden force that did not catch the eye. Look to the twisted vine tree. That was where they usually kept prisoners.

The withering was too strong for Demetrius to take them straight there. Near was possible. A start, with time running out.

Sabriel favored striking the Fanewraith outside Alula. Fern gave Elowen cake in thanks.

One more piece. Elara's control reached the top of the council. Some turned and corrupted. Not all. Help might still exist, if it could be found and trusted.

---

## The path forward

Demetrius began digging a tunnel toward the twisted tree. Time to finish. A way to reach Theodore before sunrise and the executions.

The session ended there. The party divided but connected. Fanewraith named. Influence mapped. Theodore's likely prison known. Sunnar still coming.
