# Daggerheart – Session 18 Notes

**Title:** _Eyes in the Web_  
**Theme:** The party splits on a darkening road. Prayer remakes a battlefield. Rain, spiders, and something still watching fill the gap between them.  
**Setting:** The site where [[Fulg, Stacker of Stones|Fulg]] fell, then the forest paths and wagon trail toward the grove, as storm clouds gather and the Witherwild's hold briefly thins where [[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]] prayed.

---

## Prayer at the cairn

Sabriel told [[Gnirth]] to follow [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]] while he made sure Fulg would not return. He set his sword in the ground, drew out his prayer beads, and prayed. He believed the gods made a bottleneck in the circle of life, never dying, only growing stronger, while mortals turned through birth and death as they should. He prayed that Fulg might pass on and not be reborn.

Gold light rose from him, then broke into an explosion of life. Bodies around him began to decay. Green growth took their place. He touched the rocks and they returned to earth. He saw a vision of this place as it stood in the spirit world, and felt the link between the mortal ground and the spirit that had been warping it sever.

A stone rolled away. He let it go. He lit a torch and wrote "12 hours" beside it for [[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]] and [[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]] to find.

---

## Talisman and thunder

Gnirth found the hole Fianna had been dragged down. Her talisman lay on the floor. When he picked it up, clouds covered the sun and the day dimmed. The further he went toward the village, the darker it grew.

He pushed hard to catch her, checking his back as he ran. Thunder rolled ahead. The sky flashed and blinded him with a vision of a whirlpool. He came back to himself, brushed the dirt off, and kept moving.

Fianna galloped through the forest with a rumble closing behind her. She took owl form and used roots to catapult above the canopy. As she rose, something erupted from the ground and nearly caught her. It missed. Rain touched her wings.

---

## Half a day behind

Snicket copied a map, thinking of the coin it might bring. Signs along the trail said they were on the right path, about half a day behind the others. In the distance, clouds gathered.

Hours later she and Fern reached Sabriel's mark. Rain fell hard, but the torch still burned. The flora around them looked closer to what the land had been before the Witherwild. They said as much. The travellers with them said the same, and began to speak of their youths.

They found the hole Fianna had been taken down. Something soft and glistening stuck to their faces as they passed. Fern reached up. Stringy. Rain dripped from cobwebs overhead.

Snicket cut them with her dagger. Lightning flashed and showed the silhouettes of many spiders. She shouted to the wagon driver that they needed to move. Fern answered that they did not need to be fast, only faster than whoever came behind. The wagon surged, then bogged in thick webbing.

---

## Spiders on the road

Snicket cast Hypnotic Shimmer. Shadows climbed into the webs in swirling patterns and struck two of the spiders. The people on the wagon began to panic and tried to get off.

Snicket and Fern fought as a pair. Shadow blades rose around Snicket. Fern threw her corrosive brew. The blades cut poison into the spiders and did severe damage, then again, hitting all three.

One spider bit Snicket. Another webbed Fern with spiked strands that tore her leg and tried to haul her in. The third saw her held and wrapped her legs, then bit, leaving a wither mark. Lightning flashed. Snicket vanished and reappeared behind the spider that had struck Fern, both daggers taking its head.

The remaining spider bit Fern again and left another withered mark. Fern broke free. Snicket cut that spider's legs and killed it. Fern melted the last with corrosive projectiles.

Combat ended. Eyes still watched from the dark behind them.

They cut the horse loose, freed the people, and sent the wagon charging on.

---

## Split roads

Fianna reached the grove.

Sabriel set out to catch Gnirth.

The session ended there, with the party scattered across rain and road, a lit torch counting twelve hours behind them, and eyes still waiting in the webs.
