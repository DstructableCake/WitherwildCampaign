## Sun Cycle Reminder

This world has 7 days of sun, 7 days of Nightveil. The party starts at **Day 6 of the sun cycle** and must reach Whitefeather by sunset on **Day 7** (edge of Nightveil beginning).

**Nightveil effects:** Pale bioluminescent flowers bloom. Vision becomes eerie and strange. Travel becomes harder. Danger intensifies.

**The party cannot save Whitefeather Circle.** Success is measured in people brought out alive. Say this to yourself before you start.

**Loss state:** Ritual completes, Cage Blade hits 0 three+ times, Kin dead in numbers.

## Scene 2: False Fulg — Beat Order

**Who is this?** Not Fulg. Something riding the marker stones. Possession puppet.

**Phase 1 (1–2 exchanges):** Sounds like Fulg. Curt, literal, structural. Uses 2 authentic Fulg lines, then pause.

**Phase 2 (slip it in):** Wrong notes—uses Theodore's name, knows Fionnula, praises violence, says "two cuttings" with appetite. **Instinct 12 or Knowledge 13 to catch it.**

**Phase 3 (called out):** Fanewraith's real voice leaks from smoke, not stone mouth. Tests. Offers bait specific to each PC. Does not monologue.

**Phase 4 (end):** Attack (3 HP, Diff 11, 1d8+2), flee (Agility 12 or 1 Stress), or linger (figure lunges then collapses).

**Parting line:** *"Run home. See what I left for you."*

**Fern's sprout:** Twists, hungry and repulsed. If pulled back: 1 Stress, advantage on one Knowledge/Instinct roll in Scene 4.

## Scene 2: The Possession Tells (Memorize for Scene 4)

Use these signs consistently so players recognize the chain:

- Head turns **after** body
- Footsteps land **late**
- Voice from **smoke in air**, not lips
- Mouth does not match words
- At Major damage: body drops **empty**, like cut strings

## Scene 4: Siege — Core Flow

### Zones & Teams

| Zone | Default Team | Enemies | Primary Roll |

| **Bridge (D)** | Sabriel | 3 Bladed Guard (Fear spend) | Presence/Instinct 13 per batch |

### Rotation Order Each Round

**Cage → Council Tree or Ring → Bridge or Mask. Tick clocks between spotlights.**

### Each Zone's Special Rule

**Cage:** Shaela's song lowers Cage Blade tick rate if free and unsilenced. Root tunnel to bridge once Shaela freed (Snicket Instinct 12).

**Council Tree:** Fern hedge-brace (Knowledge 13) grants +1 Grove Falling Apart tick once. Sabriel iron problem: Instinct 11 to notice; shed armour (Finesse 12, one round vulnerable) or +1 collapse tick.

**Ring:** Three anchor stones (Knowledge/Instinct 14 each). All three cracked = ritual paused, clock −2. **Fianna counter-ritual** (owl shrine custom, Presence 14): clock −1, 1 Hope. **Gnirth's temptation:** Fear spend = Fanewraith offers **[FILL FROM BACKSTORY]** to leave one stone uncracked.

**Bridge:** Sabriel Presence/Instinct 13 per three hostages. Grove Falling Apart ≤ 1 = bridge sways (Agility 12 or Restrained).

### Possessed Tavore

Use Scene 2 tells throughout. At Major damage: body drops empty, one lucid line to Sabriel: *"I did not choose first."*

**Optional grief lever:** Fianna speaks Elara Voss's name + lost shrine story. Fanewraith cannot hostile-act against her 1 round.

### End Condition

When 3 of 4 objectives met:

> [!QUOTE]
> Last child on bridge. Tree silences. Ring smokes. Sun touches hill. Nightveil rises. Run.

## Scene 5: Escape — Beat Order

1. **Bridge cross:** Presence/Instinct 13 per batch of three. Failure = Agility 12 on span.
2. **Pursuit wave:** 2 Skulkers + 2 Brambles. One exchange only.
3. **Snicket's thread:** Fanewraith's voice uses a name Snicket never told anyone (tied to Jessa's scroll origin). Plant only; do not resolve.

5. **Wither-Touched Stag (conditional):** Grove Falling Apart ≤ 2 + Fear spend. Fianna mercy choice (spare = Rohkin seed).
6. **Rohkin safety net (if needed):** Two PCs badly hurt, hostages not clear + Grove 0, or stag spared. Holds line 1–2 rounds.

**Rohkin's line:** *"The herd endures. Do you? Run."* He does not save the grove.
