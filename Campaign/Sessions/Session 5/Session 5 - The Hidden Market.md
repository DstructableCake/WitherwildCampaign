# Daggerheart – Session 5 Notes

**Title:** _Veins of the Fen_  
**Theme:** Safety thins. Nightveil's twilight pulls at hidden doubts. The wild takes its first clear hold.  
**Setting:** Onset of Nightveil's seven-day twilight, from [[Monks Eliegh]] into the border fens. The first two or three days of travel, stretching toward [[Low Passage]] and a glimpse of the Riverwalkers. Perpetual starry dusk. Bioluminescent fungi. Mists veiling the Witherwild's advance. Haven's patrols thin. Wild whispers grow bold.

---

## Chapel recovery

In the dim nave of Monks Eliegh's chapel, the long rest drew to a close amid the faint scorch marks left by [[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]]'s shattered summoning circle. Fern, marked by permanent dark stains beneath her eyes like shadowed tears, shared what she had seen: the choking gasoline, the clinging earthly bile, and the vast black ocean that ended in a thundering warning of inevitable return.

[[Mathias Vanta|Mathias]] listened, then urged a message to [[Ambrose Salecia|Ambrose]] in [[Haven]]. [[Bolgin, Bringer of the Black Marsh|Bolgin]]'s resurgence, he said, was a dire sign. [[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]], drawing from fragments of ancient lore, spoke of [[Shun'Aush, the Granit Ophid|Shun'Aush]], the granite ophid who once ruled the northern Witherwild. That led the group into a broader debate: the gods' benevolence against Haven's rulers, divine favor versus mortal hierarchy, and the moral cost of power in a world of imbalance.

Before setting out, Sabriel arranged for guards to watch over Monks Eliegh, easing Mathias' vigil and earning quiet thanks. Whispers among the others turned to [[Gnirth]]'s failed commune with a shattered statue. Its lack of memories fueled theories that the petrified might linger in limbo, a thought that stirred both hope and horror. With rations from Mathias, etched with simple wards for the road, the party stepped into Nightveil. The chapel receded. Starry dusk claimed the fen.

---

## Hunter's camp

The journey pressed on under Nightveil's unending starry veil. Paths wound through reeds that leaned in with subtle menace, their blooms unfurling as pale guides in the perpetual dusk. Two days blurred in the hush. Mists swallowed Haven's distant patrols and left black-veined prints like scars on the mud. Nocturnal calls wove into the wind's low murmur. Beauty flickered in the blooms' dance, light tracing fleeting patterns on the water. Distant howls and shifting shadows kept the undercurrent.

A campsite broke the gloom ahead. Lanterns swayed around a mired wagon. Cages rattled with muffled distress. Shadows cloaked the rough figures by the fire. [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]] slipped forward in stealth to observe. [[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]], Gnirth, and Fern approached as travelers seeking warmth. Sabriel held back.

The hunters revealed their trade: caged wildlife twisted by Witherwild taint, beasts bearing black veins and pleading eyes, bound for Haven's border labs in pursuit of Sickness cures through rumored experiments. While distraction held, Fianna unbolted a cage. The creatures spilled out in a clamor that shattered the night.

Arrows flew in retaliation. The group scattered into the woods, slipped the hunters' pursuit through the mists, and regrouped on the trail. The moral weight of the act lingered. The freed beasts' distant cries faded. Fianna's protective instincts had been affirmed. Repercussions from those who profit in the dark remained an open question.

---

## Low Passage and the night market

Deeper into Nightveil's shadows, Low Passage opened as a yawning maw of tangled roots and star-reflected pools. Its tunnels were braced by creaking wood and etched with slyborne runes that flickered under bloom-light. Snicket's eyes traced the hidden markers and guided the party through narrow, flooded passages where a wrong step meant deeper water or a root snare.

The market emerged in a cavernous root-hall. Stalls of woven reed perched on platforms, lit by swaying bloom-lanterns amid muffled barters and dripping echoes.

They bought several potions for handfuls of gold each: Fenmoss Poultice to regain health and restore stress during rests, Dew of Oove to remove minor conditions and resist Wither damage, and Reedwine Draught for social bolstering with a crash afterward. Other wares on offer included a Stride Potion for a +1 bonus to the next Agility roll, an Enlighten Potion for Knowledge, Minor Health to clear 1d4 HP, Minor Stamina to clear 1d4 Stress, a Control Potion for Finesse, and a Bolster Potion for Strength. Weapons and armor for sale ran to improved versions of current armor, a parrying dagger, a returning axe, a spiked shield, Harrowbone Armor, and Runetan Floating Armor.

Sabriel traded tales of the wilds' perils for warnings of sparse flora ahead and brittle tensions at the divide. Attacks were a real risk. Snicket worked through the ledgers and found tampered records of Veil distributions that hinted at hidden diversions.

Deeper alcoves held greater risks: a locked chest humming with wards and guarding relics that whispered power, cipher scrolls, and taint-vials that promised advantages against the dark. The market's slyborne guardians watched as the party gathered their finds and pressed on.

The session ended there, potions bought and ledgers compromised, still in the root-hall market with Nightveil's road waiting beyond.
