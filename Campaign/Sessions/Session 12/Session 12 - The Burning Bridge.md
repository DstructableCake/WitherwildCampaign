# Daggerheart – Session 12 Notes

**Title:** _The Burning Bridge_  
**Theme:** Escape costs more than steel. A hole in a prison wall can still set a city screaming.  
**Setting:** The twisted prison tree beneath [[Alula]], then the bridges and ground paths that lead back toward [[Elowen]], while high above [[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]] and [[Gnirth]] bleed for every step against [[Tavore Paran|Tavore]] and her guard.

---

## The wall gives way

[[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]] poured acid into the breach, eating at stone and root until the wall finally tore open. The fight that had already chewed through the prison floor kept finding new teeth. A guard seized [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]], dragged her into another room, and held her fast. [[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]] answered with shadow, binding both guards while Fianna was still in their grip. A third ran past and swung at Snicket. The blow missed.

Fianna and Snicket struck together. Poison finished what the hold had started. When the struggle ended, several guards lay dead, the floor of the prison was broken open, and the wall to the outside was gone.

---

## Beanstalk and alarm

[[Theodore Underbrush|Theodore]] made it through the hole, and so did others. A child. An old woman. A man whose eyes Fianna had pecked in the chaos of escape. They climbed out on a beanstalk while alarms rose through the canopy and woke the whole treetop village.

Archers on the bridge overhead loosed down at them. Fern answered with fire. The span caught, burned, and collapsed. The party and the freed prisoners reached the ground. The prisoners scattered into the undergrowth. The three who had broken the tree turned for the passage hidden in the bush, the one that led back toward Elowen.

---

## Fire in the scholars' heights

Elsewhere, Gnirth and Sabriel were still locked with Tavore and the dozen guards at her back. Round after round of combat filled the chamber with thorns and flame until everything around them burned. Guards died. Tavore did not. She fled through the broken window before the killing blow could land.

Sabriel turned to the remaining guards and tried to turn steel into words, to persuade them to help.

The session ended there, with Alula ringing in alarm, Theodore free on the forest floor, and Sabriel still speaking to men who had just watched their commander vanish into smoke and fire.
