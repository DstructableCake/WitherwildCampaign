# Session 13 Ideas

**Raw ideas, plot seeds, and "what if" beats to bridge the Alula combat and seed the next phase of the campaign.**

*Integrated at the table:* [[Session 13 Prep]] (scenarios, checklist), [[Session 13 Scene 1 - The Passage to Elowen]] & [[Session 13 Scene 4 - Hearth and Rest]] (**Hovel hit** branches), [[Session 13 Scene 3 - The Council and the Archive]] & [[Session 13 Scene 5 - The Dream]] (transition / waking notes).

## Where We Left Off (Session 12)

The party is split. The prison group (Fianna, Fern, Snicket) breached the prison with heavy collateral—acid on the wall, floor and wall destroyed, several guards killed (including poison and shadow holds in the fight), alarms raised. They escaped with Theodore and **other** prisoners (among them a child, an old woman, and others who scattered into the chaos); Fern's fire **collapsed the bridge** to the prison tree. The group made for the ground and Demetrius's passage toward [[Elowen]].

The tower group (Sabriel, Gnirth) fought [[Tavore Paran]] and many guards; **thorns and fire** scorched the scholars' chamber and surroundings—not only the prison tree burns. Multiple guards fell; [[Elder Corin]] lies dead at his desk; Tavore fled through a broken window. Session ended mid-scene with Sabriel **attempting to persuade the remaining guards** (establish their exact posture when Session 13 opens). Alula is evacuating or in chaos; rumour will lag behind truth.

## Council & Town Reaction (Gnirth & Sabriel)

- **Blame split:** Rumours could paint the pair as attackers, as defenders of the archive, or as scapegoats for the Mask's brutality. [[Elder Kaelen]] may want to hear them out; [[Elder Rowan]] may push for arrest. If Kaelen (or [[Elder Mirra]]) is swayed → council chambers access becomes possible; if Rowan dominates → arrest or flee.
- **Evidence in the chamber:** Corin's body and papers can implicate *him*—serpent coin, coded notes, execution order with his signature. If Gnirth/Sabriel secure or reveal this, it reframes who the real traitor was and turns council reaction into a choice rather than automatic hostility.
- **Remaining guards:** The guards Sabriel was persuading might be terrified of the Mask's return, loyal to the council, or loyal to the rebellion. Their choice (stand down, arrest, or look the other way) gates whether the pair can move toward the council at all.
- **[[Lirael]] (Guard-Captain):** From [[Alula]], she keeps order and resents the Mask. She can be the key contact: "I need to know what happened in that chamber before I decide what to do with you." Opens a non-combat path if the pair present evidence and don't escalate.

## Evacuation & Non-Combat Path to Council Chambers

- **Trigger:** Two trees on fire (prison area + bridge), alarms, evacuation. If Sabriel and Gnirth **do not attack further**, the village's focus on fire and safety justifies a low-alert path to the Sun-Lofts.
- **Chaos as cover:** Evacuation routes, wounded, and firefighting pull guards and civilians away from the upper council tree. The pair can move with refugees or use side paths (rope bridges, lifts) while the village focuses on fire and safety.
- **Condition:** "No further attacks" is the price—reinforce that avoiding more bloodshed keeps the "we're not the real threat" narrative and makes council/Lirael more willing to talk.
- **Optional obstacle:** One social or stealth beat—e.g. [[Lorekeeper Aelric]] or a junior guard who can raise the alarm or be persuaded/avoided. Not a full combat.
- **Success:** Reach council chambers or archive without a fight. Chambers might be partly abandoned (elders helping with evacuation) or occupied by 1–2 elders + Lirael → "what do you want / what did you find" scene.

## Fanewraith Encounter – Dream / Nightmare (Freddy Krueger Style)

- **Trigger:** First proper rest after the escape—e.g. at [[Elowen]]'s or a safe camp on the way. Whole party if reunited, or separate dreams per group that share the same "voice" and imagery (Fanewraith reaching through the weft).
- **Content:** Personal fears, guilt, or desires (from Weft Whisper: hidden weakness, secret, or fear). Environment shifts—familiar places corrupted (e.g. Serpents Hope, Stolen Fen, the prison tree). She doesn't need to appear in full form: thorns, voice, crown-shape, glimpses of Elara's face. **Survival = breaking the dream** (willpower, speaking her real name, or a memory of "what she was") rather than killing her.
- **Mechanics:** Light weight—e.g. Fear spend, Instinct or Presence rolls to resist or help an ally; 1–2 Stress or a temporary condition on failure; no full combat stats. "Survive" = wake up shaken but alive. Optional **vision** (location, warning, or taunt about the Eye) that doubles as a plot lead.
- **Tone:** Dark fantasy, personal, unsettling. Reinforce that she's watching, knows things, and is tied to the weft so a later "real" encounter feels earned.

## Variant: Ransacked or empty hovel (Session 13+)

**Use when:** You want the prison thread to arrive at **ruin instead of hearth**—violence, flight, or weft-weirdness, but **no provable fate** for [[Elowen]]. See [[Session 13 Prep]] scenario **F – Hovel hit**.

**Design goals:**

- **Ambiguous Elowen:** The table should feel something happened; **Investigation must not confirm** abduction vs flight vs stranger causes. Hold **GM-only** truth if you want; players may never get a smoking gun.
- **Direction, not deadlock:** Still surface **2–3 clue lanes** (Demetrius, physical scene, hidden fragment, faint omen) so forks stay live: [[Session 13 Prep]] intel (Featherfall, Spire / third wane, Riverwalkers), Theodore, archive names.

**Mixed signals (examples):**

- Thorn marks (*could* be Elara) **and** half-packed blanket (*could* be flight).
- [[Demetrius]] missing, injured, or erratic—**animal behaviour does not close the case.**
- Let **skills contradict:** Survival vs Insight vs Instinct/Religion.

**Clue quick reference:**

| Lane | Ambiguous examples | Points toward |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Demetrius / trail | **Dead** (body, hearth); **missing** (fur, blood, scratches toward **hearth stone**); **note** in cache—see [[Session 13 Prep]] Part A | Same geographic hooks; emotional lever for [[Theodore Underbrush]]; not a [[Bolgin, Bringer of the Black Marsh]] cameo |
| Scene | Thorns + **odd root geometry** or salt crust; stew abandoned mid-stir | Fanewraith **or** [[Alula]] nexus pressure (thin veil)—not proof she was here in body |
| Hidden | Knot-code, water-stained verse, half-burned place name under hearth | Same geographic hooks as main prep |
| Omens | One **Faint** flare (table below)—**disturbance**, not eyewitness | Cosmic stakes; see **Spirit–Wither nexus** on [[Alula]] |

**Bolgin:** Do **not** manifest bog-god horror at the hovel for this beat—[[Bolgin, Bringer of the Black Marsh]] stays an **endgame** threat; regional stress can still be **lore** on [[Alula]].

**Dream:** [[Session 13 Scene 5 - The Dream]] still works if the party rests in **ruins, camp, or cell**—first full sleep after escape.

## Faint Divinity omens at the hovel (pick one)

**Rule:** One image, sound, or wrong sense—then gone. Dialogue = **one riddle or sentence** max. These powers are **most immediately tangled** in bloom/thorn/secrets; they **do not** prove what happened to Elowen.

| Faint | Sample omen | What it does *not* prove |
|-------|-------------|--------------------------|
| [[Hyacynis, They Who Spread Small Blossoms]] | Blossoms spring from footprints; pollen hangs **too long**; petals turn toward empty air | That Elara was physically present |
| [[Oove, the Watcher of the Night Bloom]] | Bloom opens **wrong face** or closes when watched; moonlight **pools** with no sky; stemless perfect bloom on the threshold | That Oove “knows” Elowen’s fate |
| [[Qui'Gar, Whispered Sweetness for a Thorny End]] | Thorns that **numb** hurt if you lean in; honey-rot whisper; comfort with a edge | That the struggle was only physical |
| [[Whisper, the Hidden Truth]] | Half-sentence in soot; voice from burrow quoting a **PC secret**; shadow too narrow for a body | Objective truth—Whisper trades in **unease** |
| *Optional:* [[Rohkin, the Wandering Horn]] | Horn with **no herd**; pressure in the chest; echo from wrong bearing | Which path the party must take |

**Backdrop only (optional):** [[Nikta, the Great Owl]] (wrong-season flicker) and [[Shun'Aush, the Granit Ophid]] (pale dust, stone-vein in root) as **environment**—not a Higher walking on stage.

## Pacing: stop the bleeding vs cure the patient

- **Acute:** Alula manhunt, evacuation, ritual window, localized **weft** strain after the prison break—give the table **one** stabilizing win so every session is not pure siren.
- **Mid-game foil (setpiece to prep later):** Party disrupts a **ritual anchor** ([[Thorngrowth Spire]], pilgrim track, Featherfall gate, etc.); breaks a **spirit conduit** Fanewraith was using; or restores **a piece** of Nikta’s balance without ending the war → she is **forced back**, plans slip, players feel victory **that is not finale**.
- **Chronic / cosmic:** Witherwild, Haven, her full spirit-network gambit—prophecy uses **seasons or conjunctions**, not “tomorrow dawn.” **Finale attendance:** track debts and choices on [[Finale Alliance Flags]].
