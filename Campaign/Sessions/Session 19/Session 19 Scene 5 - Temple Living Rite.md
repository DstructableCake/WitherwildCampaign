# Scene 5: Living Rite at the Temple

*Scene Type:* Ritual | Combat | Social  
*Checkpoint:* Raid Task 4

---

## Goal

Disrupt the Living Rite at **(17/18)** without sacrificing bound Kin. This completes one raid task when the rite is paused or controlled through crack/swap/counter actions.

---

## Environment

The Temple of Nikta stands above stepped offering terraces. Rain runs down carved owl pillars. Three anchor points are active: center inside the temple threshold, and two on the terrace edges in the Offering Gardens.

Green-white fire pulses in synchronized beats. Each bright flare rattles hanging offerings and pushes wind outward.

From here, the roots of **(19) Great Tree** are visible climbing toward **(20) Nikta's Nest**.

## Living Rite Rules

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Bright beat action | Safe window for crack/swap |
| Off-pulse strike | Ritual clock -1 and nearest Bound Voice harmed |
| 3 on-pulse anchor successes | Rite paused; Ritual clock -2 |
| Free without swap | Bound voice freed, but Ritual clock -1 and next Fear escalates |
| Swap vessel | Bound voice freed with no hunger surge |
| Kill Tavore while all 3 bound | Ritual surge: Ritual -2 and Serious harm to one Bound Voice |

Counter-rite:
- Fianna can run *two cuttings* once (Presence 14) for Ritual -1 and 1 Hope.

## Non-Combat Paths

### Turn Tavore

Multi-beat Presence exchange, strongest with Sabriel.

- First success: hesitation (one round command drop)
- Second success: she moves to break a binding
- At this point trigger Scene 7 reclaim pressure

### Vessel swap path

- Capture/convince a loyal devotee (Presence 13 or subdue)
- Move vessel into anchor on bright beat (Strength/Finesse 14)
- Then release original bound Kin

### Precision ritual lane

- Knowledge/Instinct 13 to read beat
- Knowledge/Strength/Instinct 14 per anchor crack
