# Daggerheart – Session 1 Notes

**Title:** _Attack on Stolen Fen_  
**Theme:** Beauty before the fall. First connections. Something twisted under the quiet.  
**Setting:** Late Sunnar, late afternoon before Nightveil, at the settlement of [[Stolen Fen]]. Quiet industry, and shadows gathering.

---

## The warmth before the storm

The air held the last breath of Sunnar. The sun hung low and spilled gold across the land, turning every shadow long and soft. The wind carried wild mint and damp earth. Far off, the slow creak of wooden joints marked the Clanks at work in the fields. Tall, deliberate shapes moved among rows of white flowers, metal frames glinting. The blooms swayed like a pale tide, petals catching the light as if spun from silk. Beyond them the land dipped into the darker greens and browns of Stolen Fen, where the air grew heavy and the ground remembered every step.

It was a beautiful day. Stillness sat on it all the same, the kind that comes before something changes.

---

## Converging paths

### Stolen Fen settlement

[[Stolen Fen]] sat on stilts above marshy ground. Wooden walkways creaked underfoot. Rope bridges linked the low buildings. The market square bustled with traders, Clank overseers, and locals carrying baskets of white flowers. Peat smoke and drying herbs hung in the air.

From the edge of the fields, [[Sabriel Arden|Sabriel]] saw Captain Thorne. Broad-shouldered, weathered, his faded Haven coat hanging open. The old friend caught his eye and beckoned him over. Thorne had taken to drink. His voice was gruff and low, but he wanted to talk about the lay of the land, to open up. Sabriel, caught up in his travels toward [[Monks Eliegh]], declined to stay long. The exchange was brief, heavy with what went unsaid.

In the market square, [[Delilah Goldtooth]] waved [[Snicket Surefoot|Snicket]] over. Tall, broad, gold-capped tusks, a bright scarf. She sorted bundles of dried herbs, eyes sharp and knowing. They spoke around the stalls. Delilah tasked Snicket with delivering a small package to a shelled Galapa called [[Mr. Tilde|Mr Tilde]] in Alula. Payment on delivery. It felt like business as usual, and like trust built on years of careful trades.

### The border brush

In the undergrowth at the border the air was damp and cool. Shafts of sunlight cut the canopy and caught on white blooms that grew even this far from the fields. Insects hummed without pause, rising and falling like a strange chorus.

[[Fern Wingsteria|Fern]] and [[Fianna Fernwhisperer|Fianna]] had been travelling for a few cycles by then, seeking Brindle, Fianna's companion. He had succumbed to a virulent sickness that had been attacking wildlife deep in the [[WitherWild|Witherwild]], blackening the body and changing the mind. Through the Fungril network Fern felt a sudden, sharp tingle. Twisted power, familiar and wrong. At first she thought it was her companion. It was not. The sensation was close, and it disturbed her.

Fianna noticed a patch of flowers with petals curled inward, as if recoiling from something unseen. A small imbalance. It spoke to her tie with the living world.

Alone in the brush, [[Gnirth]] felt the warped hum of the Fungril network, and another presence drawing closer. It stirred something deep and uneasy in him. When Fern linked onto a disturbed being nearby and followed it, they came across Gnirth alone in the thicket. After talking, they chose to travel together. Gnirth had no memories of his past. A blank that both intrigued and worried his new companions.

---

## Convergence at the settlement

The rope bridges and wooden walkways of Stolen Fen came into view as the travellers approached. Peat smoke mixed with the sweet tang of white flowers. In the market square Sabriel and Snicket spotted three figures coming from the brush. Two travellers and a lone Fungril.

Before greetings could be exchanged, they heard it. A low vibration in the air, like a bow drawn across a giant string. It grew louder, sharper, until the frantic splash of feet in shallow water joined it.

---

## The Bloodwing attack

From between the reeds they saw them. Three teenagers, faces pale with fear, running for the settlement. Behind them the air shimmered with movement. Long, needle-like proboscises. Glistening wings. Bodies the size of hounds. The Bloodwings were almost on them.

The teens tried to gain entry. Two guards turned them away as strangers who should stay in the forest. Sabriel walked up behind them, ignored the quarry, and went ahead. Then the teenagers' screams split the air as they broke from the woods, five Bloodwings hard behind.

Sabriel, close by on his pilgrimage, dove into the swarm and cut severe damage across the giant bugs. The others followed. Weapons found purchase in chitinous hides. One creature was ripped apart, save the head, which fell with a wet thud.

Pheromones leaked into the woods. A cry for help. Two more Bloodwings fell. The earth rumbled. From the depths rose two further Bloodwings and their Queen, her massive form climbing from the fen.

The fight was hard. The air thick with the hum of wings and the stench of ichor. The party held the line and kept the teens behind them, blades flashing in the late sun. In the end an improvised incendiary attack turned the tide. Fire took the last bugs. Wings caught like paper. Bodies collapsed into ash.

---

## Aftermath and acceptance

The last of the Bloodwings fell or fled. The hum faded into the distance. The teenagers were shaken but alive, clutching each other and thanking the party breathlessly. Captain Thorne strode over and clapped Sabriel on the shoulder. Relief, and something darker under it. Gratitude edged with the sense that this was only the beginning.

Because of the battle and the aid given to the teens, the party gained entry to the town and sought shelter at the local inn, The Last Coil, owned by the barkeep [[Tom Yarug]].

Tom was a local elf, and a survivor of the war on Haven's side. He had not seen the front line. He had given shelter, food, and drink to those who needed it, on both sides. He preferred not to speak of what had happened. He would listen, though. A quiet presence in a world that had seen too much.

The party sold some bits from the battle. After a strong turn of bargaining, Snicket secured multiple handfuls of gold, more than enough for a week's stay. The coins felt warm in their hands.

Fianna heard that others who matched her grove might have been seen heading toward the barracks. That lay on the way to Monks Eliegh. It sounded like the direction they would take soon. A travelling cart was bound there in a few days. They could ride with it for support.

They took a long rest that covered those days. The settlement repaired the damage. The Clanks returned to their work. The hum in the air faded, but it did not vanish. Each of them had found a reason to linger in Stolen Fen, for business, duty, or curiosity. For now there was rest.

The session ended there, under the last light of Sunnar. A week of rest ahead. A cart bound for Monks Eliegh. And a hum in the air that had not quite gone.
