# Scene 1: Wash-Up in the Weft

*Scene Type:* Arrival | Orientation | Immediate Threat  
*Checkpoint:* Teach realm logic and launch pressure

---

## Goal

Re-anchor the party after the orb implosion, establish realm rules, and end with the unseated Leshen already rising from the water.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

You are not in a cave, a dream, or a heaven. You are on a **shore**.

The beach is packed white owl-feathers, wet as snow, deep enough that boots sink to the ankle. Each feather is the size of a hand. When you pick one up it is warm, then cold, then gone — a name you almost remember.

The water is black glass. It does not chop. It breathes. It holds the aurora like spilled oil: purple light, moving stars, and the mortal world turning far below — continents, a glitter of city-lights, the slow white of mountain-snow. You cannot pick a grove out of that map. If you reach for it your hand comes back dry and ringing.

The sky is the same vision they already saw when the orb took them. There is no sun and no canopy-roof. A **purple aurora** fills the vault. Through it, stars and whole rivers of the universe move. The mortal realm is laid out as if they stood on the top of the world and looked down: landmasses, ocean-swells, ranges, the glow of cities. No faces. No Whitefeather Circle. Just the shape of the living world, too far to climb to.

Inland, a line of pale trees stands where a dune should be. Every trunk throws **two shadows**. One copies you. The other copies something you did yesterday, or might do next. Between the trees, river-stone cairns sit in Riverwalker shapes, humming very softly.

Far out on the black water, once, a driftwood staff rings against a hidden ford. Then the ring is gone.

---

## How You Travel

You do not leave this shore by walking "inland" as a map would have it.

- **Walk toward need.** Name the thing you want under your breath (home, water, a crossing, each other) and the beach will slope that way. Walk toward a compass point and the shore loops.
- **Fern is the compass.** A Way feels like a cold seam under the sternum, pulling toward later water.
- **Puddles are the same sky, closer to the hand.** They show aurora and continents, not a street in the grove. Stepping into one does not take you home. It takes a bite of you.
- **Do not swim.** The black water is memory-deep. Wading to the knees is possible. Past that, lungs fill with other people's last breaths.
- **The Leshen is already here.** Travel talk is short. Antlers break the water before anyone has finished coughing.

## Player Actions

Give each PC one fast beat, then the Leshen is up. Any two of the following count as "oriented":

| Approach | What they can do | Roll / Cost | What they learn |
|----------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Investigate** | Look up through the aurora, or into black water | Instinct or Knowledge 13 | Continents, waves, mountains, city-lights; no grove-level detail; they are above the mortal world, not beside it |
| **Investigate** | Taste the air / water without drinking | Instinct 14 | This is [[The Weft]], not death; red-flower metal-tang from Lyra's smoke |
| **Social** | Speak a true name to call the scattered party together | Presence 13 | They arrive as one group despite lane split; on Fear, something else answers too |
| **Social** | Call for [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]] | Presence 14 | Staff-ring far out; he will not ferry while the Leshen claims the shore |
| **Stealth** | Stay low in feather-silt and read the Leshen before it fully rises | Agility 14 | Two crowns, pinned warriors still in its roots, physical blades will bite less here |
| **Combat** | Strike the rising antlers early | Standard attack | It is [[Leshen Unseated]]; starting the fight early skips leftover orientation |

**Do not:**

- Drink the black water or eat a warm feather (ticks **Living Tether**).
- **Try to climb the aurora-world home** (mark 1 Stress; continents stay the size of a map).

Start **Living Tether 6** and **Hunger Pursuit 5** on the table now.

## Read-Aloud

> The beach is white with owl-feathers. The water is black as polished obsidian, holding purple light like oil.  
> Overhead, the sky is the aurora you already know: stars and universe in motion, the mortal world spread beneath it as if you stood on the roof of creation. Continents. Mountains. City-lights. No grove you can point to.  
> Antlers break the surface first. The Leshen from the elder platforms rises with you, twice-crowned now, roots drinking the black water as if this shore had always been its grove.

## Immediate Situation

- The party arrives together despite lane separation in the mortal grove.
- The [[Leshen Warden]] came through with Fern and Snicket. Use [[Leshen Unseated]] from here.
- No one is dead, but everyone feels slightly misaligned with body and voice.
- The shore itself reacts to emotion; fear and naming cause visible ripples.
- Distant staff-rings on the water hint at [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]] watching.

---

## Realm Rules to Teach

State plainly in-scene:

- Names have weight and consequences here.
- Distance follows intent, not map geometry.
- Spirit food and water can erode memory.
- The living burn bright to spirit predators.
- Crossing home requires tribute and a stable seam.

---

## Senses

- **Sight:** Feather-silt beach; black water holding the purple aurora; two shadows per tree; mortal continents and city-lights turning in the sky, too far for detail.
- **Sound:** Shore hiss, distant chain-rattle, heartbeats in wet bark, one far staff-ring.
- **Smell:** Wet moss, iron, crushed mint, old smoke.
- **Touch:** Cold air and warm skin at once; feathers clinging to wrists; subtle drag on every step.
- **Taste:** Metallic tang when speaking true names.

---

## Fear Usage

- Spend 1 Fear to make one PC hear their own name from the waterline.
- Spend 1 Fear to tick **Living Tether** if someone tests spirit water carelessly.
- Spend 1 Fear to make the Leshen's second crown turn toward a named PC.
