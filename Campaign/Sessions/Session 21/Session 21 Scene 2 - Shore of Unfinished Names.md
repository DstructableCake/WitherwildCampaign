# Scene 2: Shore of Unfinished Names

*Scene Type:* Opening Combat | Continuity Boss  
*Checkpoint:* Finish the Leshen fight that started in the grove

---

## Goal

Resolve the Leshen that was yanked through with Fern and Snicket, now powered by the Weft, and teach spirit-realm combat without replacing that unfinished duel.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

The beach has become a battlefield the Leshen is **growing**.

Where its roots touch feather-silt, the white feathers blacken and braid into root-mats. Pinned grove warriors hang in those mats like fruit, faces gone thin as reflections — still the people from the elder platforms, already half-translated into Bound Voices of this place.

The Leshen is twice the height it held in the rain. One antler-crown is bone and bark, dripping Weft-water. The second crown is pale, a breath late, made of the grove's memory of it. Hitting the pale crown is like cutting fog. Behind it, a single [[Weft Warden]] watches from the dune-trees as if this claim were already legal.

Black water laps in a perfect circle around the fight, a ring of oil-mirrors. In those mirrors the same purple aurora turns, and the mortal world is a map of continents and lights. If a PC is slammed into the water they see no elder platform — only the world from above, too far, and the Leshen's second crown doubled in the reflection.

Seam-moths hatch from cracks in the air where the orb's yank tore the world. The Drowned Chorus does not walk up the beach; it **stands up out of the reflections**, singing names the Leshen has heard.

Inland, past the two-shadow trees, a pale road is already assembling itself from footprints that have not been taken yet. That is the exit, but the Leshen is making this shore into a grove first.

---

## How You Travel (During the Fight)

- **Flank through feather-dunes.** Soft, slow, but it beats the memory-crown (Agility 14 to move without sinking).
- **Use the waterline.** Close range to the Leshen's roots, Far from its pale crown — Gnirth's water-read helps. Wading past the knees is a Tether risk.
- **Climb a two-shadow tree.** Height lets you call which crown is real (Instinct 16), and the aurora-world is closer to the eye but still without grove-detail. The Warden owns the high branches.
- **Do not run inland yet.** The road will not hold a passenger while the Leshen still claims the shore. If someone tries, the beach loops them back into Close range of the antlers.
- **Mercy path is movement too.** Reaching a pinned Echo to **Sever the Brand** is a Close-range action, not only a damage race.

## Player Actions

This is a fight, but it is not only a fight. Any of these can sit on a spotlight:

| Approach | Action | Roll | Effect |
|----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Combat** | Strike the living crown | Attack after Instinct/Knowledge 16, or accept disadvantage | Standard damage; pale crown wastes the hit |
| **Combat** | Cut a root-mat to free an Echo by force | Strength 17 or Severe nonlethal | Frees one Echo; Leshen loses some Pinned Host grip |
| **Social / Mercy** | Speak grove-duty or unbind the brand | Presence, Instinct, or Knowledge 17 (**Sever the Brand**) | Frees one Echo without killing it; full free = kneel/flee option |
| **Investigate** | Read which crown is real this round | Instinct or Knowledge 16 | Next attack this round ignores Two Crowns |
| **Investigate** | Ask the black water what the Leshen wants | Instinct 14 | It wants a grove. This shore will do. It will not share living heat |
| **Stealth** | Circle through feather-dunes to an Echo | Agility 15 | Start your next action at that Echo without drawing Root Binding |
| **Social** | Call the watching Warden off as "trespass" | Presence 16 | On success, the Warden delays one spotlight; on Fear, it joins early |

Cairn after victory: each PC clears 1 Stress **or** 1 HP, not both.

## Encounter Logic

- This is the same Leshen from [[Session 20 Scene 4 - Elder Platforms and Antlers]], not a new spirit-beast.
- [[Leshen Unseated]] is home here: physical resistance, two crowns, and Stress recovery on fail-with-Fear.
- Echoes are the pinned grove warriors. **Pinned Host** lasts until they are freed or dropped.
- **Sever the Brand** still works (Difficulty 17). Mercy remains a real path.
- Chorus, Warden, and Moths are the Weft answering its claim, not a second boss.
- Fern and Snicket should feel the unfinished platform fight; the rest of the party is joining mid-duel.

---

## Win Conditions

Any one of these advances the party:

- Defeat [[Leshen Unseated]].
- Free all Bound-Voice Echoes via **Sever the Brand**, then drive it inland or force a kneel.
- Mark its last HP with a declared nonlethal strike after the Echoes are gone.

After success, a spirit cairn appears. Party chooses one:

- Each PC clears 1 Stress, **or**
- each PC clears 1 HP.

Do not allow both.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Two-crowned Leshen; root-mats in white feathers; black water holding purple aurora and far continents.
- **Sound:** Antler-knock, chorus of stolen names, moth-wing paper, a low cosmic hush behind the aurora.
- **Smell:** Wet bark, iron, crushed mint, sap where roots braid the beach.
- **Touch:** Feather-silt sucking at boots; root-lash cold as river-stone.
- **Taste:** Metal when a true name is sung.

## Fear Usage

- **Antler Call spike:** Spend Fear to strip Hope from two targets at once.
- **Two Crowns:** Force a wasted first attack into the memory-crown.
- **Clock pressure:** On fail-with-Fear near the Leshen, tick **Living Tether** and let Home in the Weft clear 1 Stress on it.
