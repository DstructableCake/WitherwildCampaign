# Session 21 - Set Pieces and Environments

Use this sheet to keep terrain alive, spiritual, and mechanically relevant throughout the gauntlet.

Tappable combat packages live on the scene notes and in [[Session 21 - Initiative Tracker Encounters]]. Budget assumes **4 PCs at level 5** (base 14 BP).

---

## Shared Environment Tone

- Gravity, rain, and distance are unstable and reactive.
- The sky is always the purple aurora from last session: stars and universe in motion, mortal continents and city-lights visible as if from the top of the world, never street-level detail.
- The realm echoes true names, unfinished vows, and fear spikes.
- Green-white pulse surges indicate The Hunger is near.
- Water is memory-active: reflections can reveal, lure, or wound.

## Scene 1-2 Set Piece - Shore of Unfinished Names

Use during [[Session 21 Scene 1 - Wash-Up in the Weft]] and [[Session 21 Scene 2 - Shore of Unfinished Names]].

### Environment Moves

- **Two-Crown Feint:** Instinct 16 to strike the living antlers, not the memory-crown.
- **Root Clamp:** Strength 15 or become temporarily Restrained in feather-silt.
- **Rising Rain:** Agility 13 or lose one range of movement on slick feather-silt.

### Fear Spends

- Force Antler Call against Fern and Snicket first.
- Tick **Living Tether** after fail-with-Fear inside Root Binding.
- Slide the Leshen one range without reactions as roots drink the black water.

## Cross-Session Fear Budget

- **Opening beats:** 1-2 Fear per scene.
- **Mid gauntlet:** 2-4 Fear per scene.
- **Final ford:** reserve at least 4 Fear for Hunger and crossing denial.

Use Fear to escalate position, timing, and identity pressure first; raw damage second.
