# Scene 5: The Hunger Notices

*Scene Type:* Running Encounter | Pursuit  
*Checkpoint:* Reach ford alive, no static stand

---

## Goal

Create panic momentum and attrition without stalling into a full traditional combat.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

The leaf-floor of the grove does not gently slope. It **tears**.

You are running down a wound in the world: a long descent of living roots that hang like a frozen waterfall. Some roots are bridges. Some are slides of wet bark. Some are hollow and roar with black water inside, like drainpipes made of oak. Through gaps in the roots the purple aurora still burns, the mortal world turning as continents and lights. Green-white light wells up behind you — not a creature with a location yet, a **mouth-shaped horizon** eating the grove you just left.

Three lanes present themselves, all wrong:

- **High:** narrow root-bridges, good sight, Wardens own this air.
- **Mid:** bark-slides, fast, moth-choked, easy to lose an Experience to dust.
- **Low:** a water-channel in a hollow root. Faster still. The Chorus sings from the walls. Gnirth can ride this. Anyone else risks drowning in memory.

False offshoots look like the mortal grove: a platform, a child's hand, Fionnula's voice. Those are bait. They smell sweet.

Far below, once, Lathun's staff rings on stone. That is the only true destination.

---

## How You Travel

- **Run toward "crossing," not away from the mouth.** Associative distance: fear-running "anywhere" loops you closer to the Hunger.
- **Pick a lane and commit for a round.** Switching mid-round is Agility 15 or you drop one range and mark 1 Stress.
- **Do not stop to fully kill.** One move, one obstacle, one hostile beat. If they plant and brawl, Lathun's line: *Water does not wrestle the sea. Run.*
- **Follow Fern's cold seam** and/or the staff-ring. If those disagree, the staff-ring is the ford; the seam might be a Way-fragment (investigate on the move, Instinct 14).
- **Help costs a spotlight.** Pulling a yanked ally is Strength or Agility 15. Leaving them ticks **Hunger Pursuit**.
- **Drop a tribute to buy distance** (a story shouted, a drop of blood on a root, a name you can spare). Presence 13: skip one Yank this round.

---

## Player Actions

Chase structure: each round, every PC chooses a travel action. Then one environmental move. Then one hostile beat.

| Approach | Action | Roll | Effect |
|----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Travel** | Take the High lane | Agility 15 | Stay ahead; fail and a Warden contests you |
| **Travel** | Take the Mid lane | Agility 14 | Fast; fail-with-Fear and moths mute an Experience |
| **Travel** | Take the Low lane | Instinct 14 (Gnirth advantage) | Fastest to the ford; fail and mark 1 Stress (lungs) |
| **Combat** | Clear a blocker without stopping | One attack, then you must still move | Drop an Echo or chip a Warden; you do not get a second attack |
| **Social** | Shout a false name behind you as bait | Presence 14 | Hunger Yank targets the name, not a PC, this round |
| **Stealth** | Take a darker offshoot that is not sweet | Agility 16 | Skip one Echo; on Fear it was bait anyway |
| **Rescue** | Pull a yanked ally back | Strength or Agility 15 | They stay in formation; fail ticks **Hunger Pursuit** |

[[The Hunger]] is silhouette only. Do not put its Solo sheet in this block.

## Encounter Frame

- This is a chase, not a hold-the-line fight.
- Run in fast rounds: move, obstacle, one hostile pressure beat.
- Echoes pin lanes; Wardens cut off high routes; Chorus and Moths harry the rear.
- The Hunger stays mostly silhouette pressure until Scene 6.

---

## Environmental Moves

1. **Bright Beat Theft:** A PC who rolled with Hope last spotlight must pass Presence 14 or lose their next action.
2. **Yank:** Instinct 15 or be pulled one range toward the maw.
3. **Root Break:** Agility 15 or drop one range and mark 1 Stress.

Any fail with Fear ticks **Hunger Pursuit**.
