# Scene 6: Ford of Two Worlds

*Scene Type:* Climax Hold | Escape Ritual  
*Checkpoint:* Final crossing or catastrophic failure

---

## Goal

Hold the ford long enough for [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]] to cut a stable Way home while The Hunger and Tavore's command-shadow attempt to seize it.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

The descent dumps you into a wide black sheet of water no deeper than the knee — until it is. Standing stones break the surface in a ragged circle, each taller than Sabriel, each carved with names. Some names you know (grove-kin, Riverwalkers, a Stone Serpent dead at Bolgin). Some you will know later. One stone is blank. Lathun will not say whose it is.

Overhead, the purple aurora is closer than it has been all session. Stars still move. The mortal world is still a map: continents, ocean-swell, mountain-snow, city-lights. You still cannot pick Whitefeather Circle out of it. Between the stones, the black water holds that same sky — aurora and world-map doubled, shut. That doubled map is the Way. Lathun stands in the center, a figure of river-mist and current, face never fully one face. Driftwood staff. Water runs off them and does not make them smaller.

[[The Hunger]] arrives as the grove-wound given a body: a vertical maw of green-white fire wrapped in the same root-light chains that bound voices at the altar. It does not step into the circle so much as **replace the air** at the circle's edge.

The [[Mask-That-Walks]] turns under the aurora like a commander choosing where you die. Nothing living is behind it. It still has Tavore's voice.

When the Crossing clock ticks, a patch of the world-map in the water brightens — still not street-level, but a region, a coast, a cluster of lights — as if the Way were choosing where to drop you. When it fills, that patch rushes up to become rain and trees.

---

## How You Travel (You Don't Leave Until It's Paid)

- **This is a hold, not a march.** The Way opens in the water between stones. You stay until **Crossing Hold** fills or the Tether dies.
- **Each PC claims a stone** or guards Lathun. Empty stones are where Echoes climb out.
- **Tribute is travel.** A story, a tear, a drop of blood, a vow — without it the window will not take you.
- **Matching the beat.** Lathun's staff-rings are the bright beat from the living rite, heard from this side. Act on the ring (Hope spend or a declared hold action). Act off-ring and Hunger Swallow the Flare.
- **Chasing the Mask off-circle** is a choice. It can be driven back. It cannot be used as the ferry. If Sabriel follows it out of the stones, he is not on a hold action that tick.
- **When the clock fills:** the chosen patch of the world-map rushes up. You do not climb. You **wash**. Mortal rain hits your face. Roots of the Great Tree. Hard cut.

## Player Actions

Tick **Crossing Hold** (start 4, count up to done) when someone succeeds at a hold-related action below, spends Hope to stabilize, or hits Lathun's ring on-beat.

| Approach | Action | Roll | Effect |
|----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Hold** | Brace a named stone and keep the seam open | Presence or Strength 15 | Crossing Hold +1 |
| **Hold** | Match Lathun's current with water-sense | Instinct 14 (Gnirth advantage) | Crossing Hold +1; on crit, +2 |
| **Hold** | Keep the seam visible (hedge-sense) | Instinct or Knowledge 14 (Fern advantage) | Allies have advantage on the next hold roll |
| **Combat** | Drive Hunger or Mask off a stone | Standard combat | Protects a holder; does not tick the clock by itself |
| **Social** | Pay tribute (story, tear, blood, vow) | No roll if sincere; Presence 13 if bargaining cheap | Required from each PC before the final tick; Lathun's canon price |
| **Social** | Bargain with the Mask (rival runner, not ally) | Presence 16 (Sabriel's history) | Mask delays one Hold the Beat; it still wants the Way for Fanewraith |
| **Stealth / Trick** | Hide a holder behind a blank stone | Agility 15 | Hunger Bind Voice misses that PC this round |
| **Investigate** | Read a carved name for a weapon | Knowledge 14 | You may speak that name once as a weapon (advantage on one attack) or as extra tribute |

If **Living Tether** hits 0 first, they cannot fully cross without a campaign-level cost.

## Crossing Rules

Run **Crossing Hold 4** as an upward clock.

Tick the clock up when:

- A PC succeeds at a hold action on the stones.
- A PC spends Hope in service of stabilizing the ford.
- A PC succeeds on an on-beat action keyed to Lathun's call.

If **Living Tether** reaches 0 first, the party cannot fully cross without major campaign cost.

---

## Lathun's Tribute Requirement

Before final tick, each PC contributes one crossing tribute:

- a story,
- a tear,
- a drop of blood,
- a binding vow,
- or equivalent balanced cost.

No tribute, no stable return.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Knee-deep black water; named stones; purple aurora doubled in the ford; mortal continents and city-lights, still without grove-detail; Hunger as a vertical wound; an empty mask that still commands.
- **Sound:** Staff on stone (the bright beat); chain-light; a cosmic hush behind the aurora.
- **Smell:** River-mist, iron, crushed mint, ozone from the maw.
- **Touch:** Cold current pulling toward the window; stone-grit names under the palm.
- **Taste:** Metal so thick it is almost blood — then, if you succeed, mortal rain.
