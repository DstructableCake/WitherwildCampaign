# Scene 4: Grove of Two Shadows

*Scene Type:* Hard Midpoint Combat | Bargain Branch  
*Checkpoint:* Break through wardens or pay narrative cost

---

## Goal

Deliver the hardest midpoint fight, then launch the party toward the ford with either earned momentum or costly passage.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

This is Whitefeather Circle as the Weft keeps it.

The Great Tree grows **down** — not because the sky is a roof of earth, but because this grove has inverted itself under the aurora. Its roots hang in the air like antlers of living night, framing the purple vault. Through those roots you can still see what you saw last session: stars moving, the mortal world as continents, waves, mountain-snow, city-lights. No street. No Circle. Just the map of the living world, impossibly far.

You walk on a floor of compressed gold-green leaves that crunches like frost and bleeds sap when cut.

Every standing trunk throws two shadows. One is ordinary. The other is a person-shaped stain that moves on a delay — sometimes copying you, sometimes copying the Warden you have not seen yet. Nikta's carved eye in the hanging bark is full of black water. If Fianna looks into it, the water looks back with one pupil too many.

Elder platforms exist as hollow memories: rope bridges to nowhere, treehouses with no walls, hearths that burn cold. Night-blooms open in the dark margins, each bloom a watching eye ([[Oove, the Watcher of the Night Bloom]]). They do not help unless someone keeps an oath.

At the center, bark opens along a root-face. That is [[Root-That-Remembers]]: the grove's archive, speaking like old wood. Names already live in its grain. It will take more.

The Way out is not through the hanging trunk. Fern's cold seam pulls **downslope**, toward a tear in the leaf-floor where dark water sounds far below.

---

## How You Travel

- **Treat it as a library fire.** The wardens are not bandits; they are closing stacks around living heat.
- **Move by shadow-reading.** Before you cross a clearing, pick which shadow is true (Knowledge or Instinct 14) or you step into bramble that was not there.
- **Climb the inverted roots** to get above a Warden (Agility 15). Canopy Drop will answer.
- **Speak your names or refuse to.** Naming is how the grove files you. Silence is how you stay a trespasser. Both have costs.
- **The bargain is a door.** Leaving a name in the bark is travel: the grove lets you pass and keeps a piece of you until you return.
- **Exit toward water, not toward the Great Tree.** The Tree is the judge. The ford is later, down.

## Player Actions

Complete the beat by fighting through **or** by paying. Mix is allowed.

| Approach | Action | Roll | Effect |
|----------|--------|------|--------|
| **Combat** | Break wardens and wound the Root | Standard combat | Full victory; reduce **Hunger Pursuit** by 1 |
| **Social** | Name yourselves as Feathersong / balance-keepers, not invaders | Presence 15 (Fianna advantage if grove-duty) | Skip one Warden spotlight, or open the bark bargain at better terms (one PC's Experience, not all) |
| **Social** | Accept the bark bargain | Each PC names an Experience to leave | Pass; skip Wave 2; that Experience is gone until a later return to the Weft |
| **Investigate** | Read which silhouette is true before striking | Knowledge or Instinct 14 | Ignore Two Shadows on your next attack |
| **Investigate** | Listen at Nikta's water-eye | Instinct 14 | Split-voice line for Fianna; on Fear, **Living Tether** ticks |
| **Stealth** | Slip the leaf-floor tear without a fight | Agility 16, and the table must commit | Possible only if they already paid or dropped a Warden; otherwise the Root notices |
| **Combat / Mercy** | Cut a name *out* of the bark instead of leaving one | Finesse or Knowledge 17 | Free a trapped memory (maybe a Bound Voice); Wave 2 still comes |

Optional 90-second beat, do not stop the fight: Fianna hears Nikta's dual voice. Fern sees midnight-bloom eyes.

## Encounter Logic

- Wardens lock down lane control and punish rushed attacks.
- Root-That-Remembers controls space and recycles allied force.
- Use the shadow mechanic to reward setup and attention.

---

## Bargain Branch

If party attrition is high or pacing needs acceleration, offer terms:

- Leave one name in the bark (each PC loses access to one chosen Experience until next return to the Weft), then pass. Skip Wave 2.
- Refuse and continue full combat, including Wave 2.

Both outcomes move to Scene 5.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Inverted Great Tree against purple aurora; two shadows; Nikta's eye full of black water; night-bloom pupils; continents and city-lights through the roots, never grove-detail.
- **Sound:** Wood speaking; antler-knock; moth-paper; sap ticking like a clock.
- **Smell:** Sap, rain-metal, fresh-cut bark, cold hearth-smoke with no fire.
- **Touch:** Grit of bark-dust; tug in the spine when a true name is spoken.
- **Taste:** Bitter resin in bramble zones.
