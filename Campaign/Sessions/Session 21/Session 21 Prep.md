# Session 21 Prep: The Drowned Path Home

**Theme:** Escape through a hungry spirit realm before identity collapses.  
**Setting:** [[The Weft]], the spirit-side of [[Fanewick]], immediately after the altar orb implosion.

**TL;DR:** Open on wash-up and realm rules, run a five-fight gauntlet with short attrition recoveries, and close on a hard crossing hold at Lathun's ford.

---

> [!QUOTE] [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]]

## Realm Rules (teach in Beat 1)

- Names have weight; true names can wound, bind, or invite retaliation.
- Distance is associative; walking toward intent matters more than map logic.
- Spirit food is forgetting; consuming it advances Living Tether.
- The living are warm beacons; spirit predators detect heat and heartbeat.
- Every crossing requires tribute: blood, story, tear, vow, or equivalent cost.

---

## Fear Economy (Spend Menu)

- Collapse or distort range bands in movement scenes.
- Force identity pressure: lose Hope, mute an Experience, misread a shadow.
- Escalate the clock game instead of only direct damage.
- Spawn or relay minion pressure at bad angles.
- At climax, spend for The Hunger's action economy and Mask command chains.

## Beat Packages and BP Guidance (4 PCs, Tier 3)

Base full-party budget is **14 BP** (`(3 x 4) + 2`).

- **-1** easier/shorter = **13**
- **+2** harder/longer = **16**

Spend costs: Minion group equal to party size (4) = 1, Horde/Standard/Skulk/Ranged = 2, Leader = 3, Bruiser = 4, Solo = 5.

| Beat | Target | Built | Package |

| Ford | 16 (harder) | 16 | [[The Hunger]] + [[Mask-That-Walks]] + [[Drowned Chorus]] + [[Weft Warden]] + [[Seam-Moth Swarm]] + [[Bound-Voice Echo]] x8 |

Stagger reinforcements rather than dumping the full spend on round one. Encounter blocks live on each combat scene so they can be tapped in Obsidian.

Scaling dials:

- **Too easy:** Bring the next staggered wave in immediately.
- **Too hard:** Cut the latest horde/minion wave first; keep the scene's fantasy boss.
- **Too slow:** Convert one hostile spotlight into environmental objective pressure.
- **Fifth PC sits in:** Add one Horde (+2) and one Minion group (+1).

## Branch Cards

### Leshen Continuity Branch (Beat 2)

Carry forward how Session 20 treated the [[Leshen Warden]]:

- **Mercy was in progress:** Open with **Sever the Brand** still available. The Leshen is confused, territorial, and trying to make this shore into a grove. Freeing Echoes still kneels or drives it inland.
- **Kill was in progress:** Skip the kneel option until it is bloodied. It hunts Fern and Snicket first as the ones who were on it when the orb yanked.

### Grove Bargain Branch (Beat 4)

If the party is overextended, [[Root-That-Remembers]] offers terms:

- Leave one name in the bark (temporarily lose one Experience) and pass.
- Refuse and continue full combat.

Both paths preserve momentum into Beat 5; the bargain path trades attrition for narrative debt.

### Tavore State Branch (Beat 6)

- **Default:** Run [[Mask-That-Walks]] as Tavore's command-shadow.
- **If Tavore crossed physically:** Use the mask as pressure while Tavore remains a rival runner and potential unstable ally.

## Quick GM Card

- Teach the realm rules once, then reinforce only when triggered.
- Each scene now has **look**, **travel**, and **player actions** (combat is not the only move).
- Keep clocks public; players should feel escape pressure every beat.
- Spend Fear on position, identity erosion, and pacing spikes.
- Preserve one hard recovery point between major fights, never a full reset.
- If the table is wrecked after Beat 4, skip Beat 5's encounter block and go straight to the ford with Hunger already closing.
