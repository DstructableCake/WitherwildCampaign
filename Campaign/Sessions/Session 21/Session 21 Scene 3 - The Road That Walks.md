# Scene 3: The Road That Walks

*Scene Type:* Movement Combat | Positional Puzzle  
*Checkpoint:* Mid-gauntlet displacement pressure

---

## Goal

Force fast decisions under shifting geometry while preserving party cohesion and momentum toward the grove-mirror and, beyond it, water.

---

## What This Place Looks Like

There is no gate. The shore simply **narrows** until you are standing on a road that has a spine.

The causeway is packed from things that should not be masonry: wagon-ruts from Stolen Fen, wet cobbles from a Haven street Snicket knows, Riverwalker ford-stones, owl-feathers, and names burned into mud like cattle brands. When you look down, you can read journeys — including ones you have not taken yet.

The road is a serpent. You feel it flex. Segments of landscape slide past on either side like scenery on a badly run stage: a strip of Nightveil pine, a strip of bog, a strip of Whitefeather platform, none of them connected by ordinary distance. Between segments is white seam-light, and from those seams the moths hatch.

Overhead the purple aurora still hangs: stars moving, the mortal world turning as a map of continents, waves, and city-lights. Wardens can drop from inverted branches against that sky. If you look too long at the horizon, the horizon has teeth.

Falling off the road does not mean falling into a ditch. It means landing in a **wrong league**: a memory-place that looks like home for three seconds, then tries to keep you. Getting back on is a climb up moving muscle.

---

## How You Travel

- **Stay on the spine.** Walking toward "the grove," "Nikta," "water," or "a crossing" keeps the road pointed. Walking toward a mapped town makes it shed a league.
- **Hold formation.** If the party stays within Close of each other, Devour the Between hurts less. Split and the road eats the gap.
- **Ride, don't wrestle.** You can run, cling, jump a seam (Agility 15), or lie flat when it sheds. Trying to "kill the ground" is valid — that is wounding the [[Mile-Eater]] until it vomits you at the destination.
- **Call a destination.** One PC may spend a spotlight to name the need (Presence 14). Success points the next stretch. Fear names a false destination (sweet-smelling, Tether tick if followed).
- **Fern's seam** still pulls toward later water. The grove-mirror is on the way, not the end.
- **Do not eat moth-dust or lick a name out of the mud.** That is spirit-food.

## Player Actions

Keep this moving. One travel choice and one threat per round is enough.

| Approach | Action | Roll | Effect |

| **Investigate** | Read a name in the mud | Knowledge 13 | Learn a true name you can use later as weapon or tribute; on Fear, the Chorus learns yours |

## Encounter Rules

- The road is an active hazard, not static terrain.
- Falling or being thrown off-route ticks **Living Tether** and risks isolation.
- Keep movement meaningful: players who hold formation reduce fear spikes.
- The Swarm should threaten utility and confidence by muting Experiences.

---

## Environmental Challenges

1. **Sloughing Edge:** Agility 15 to keep footing when the road sheds.
2. **Name-Wind:** Presence 14 to resist hearing a false destination.
3. **Seam Flash:** Instinct 14 to read where the next stable step appears.

On fail with Fear for any challenge, tick **Hunger Pursuit**.

---

## Win Conditions

- Survive long enough for the road to throw them at the grove approach, **or**
- force the [[Mile-Eater]] to collapse and spit the party onto the next zone.

Optional success bonus:

- If no PC was isolated for a full round, reduce **Hunger Pursuit** by 1.

## Senses

- **Sight:** Stitched landscapes sliding by; seam-light; a horizon with teeth; names in the mud; purple aurora and far city-lights overhead.
- **Sound:** Leather and cart-wood groaning, moth-paper, a low animal swallow when distance vanishes.
- **Smell:** Dust, wet pine, old horse-sweat, sweet false-destination air.
- **Touch:** The road pulse underfoot, like a throat.
- **Taste:** Grit of burned names if you fall and catch yourself with your mouth open.

---

## Fear Usage

- Spend Fear to collapse range bands during key positioning turns.
- Spend Fear to isolate one PC onto a severed road-strip.
- Spend Fear after a failed move to deal direct damage via Wrong Turn.
