# The Weft

## Overview

The Weft is the spirit-side of [[Fanewick]] where names, vows, and unfinished intent become terrain. It is not the Outer Realms and not an afterlife. The living can cross by rupture, rite, or guided passage, but the realm resists their weight and attempts to fold them into itself.

The altar orb implosion at [[Whitefeather Circle]] opened a short-lived wound into this place. The party was not killed; they were unseated from mortal rhythm and cast ashore in the Weft.

## Appearance and Senses

- Shorelines of white feather-silt and black water that holds the aurora like oil.
- Trees cast two shadows each, one moving with light and one moving with memory.
- Smell of wet bark, iron, crushed mint, and old smoke.
- Taste of coin-metal at the back of the tongue whenever a crossing is near.

### The Sky (table canon from last session)

Do not replace this with a canopy, a root-ceiling, or rain falling into leaves. The party has already seen:

- A **purple aurora** thick as weather.
- Stars and whole currents of the universe moving behind and through it.
- The **mortal realm visible as if looking down from the top of the world**: continents, mountain chains, ocean-swells, city-lights.
- **No low-level detail.** They cannot pick out a grove, a face, a house, or Whitefeather Circle. Haven is a cluster of lights. Fanewick is a green-dark continent-shape. The sea moves.

Local Weft terrain (shore, road, grove, ford) sits *under* that sky. Water and aurora may mirror each other. Neither is a street-level window home.

## Session 21 Geography

The Weft does not sit beside Fanewick. It is Fanewick remembered, hungered, and stacked wrong, with the living world turning in the aurora overhead. For this session, the party moves through five linked countries of one country:

1. **The Shore of Unfinished Names** — a feather-beach under the purple aurora, where the living wash up.
2. **The Road That Walks** — a living causeway stitched from every path they have ever taken, including ones they have not taken yet.
3. **The Grove of Two Shadows** — Whitefeather Circle as [[Nikta, the Great Owl]]'s weft remembers it: the Great Tree grows down, every trunk throws two shadows.
4. **The Falling Lanes** — the grove tearing open into a descent of root-bridges while [[The Hunger]] closes from behind.
5. **The Ford of Two Worlds** — [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]]'s standing stones in black water, the only stable seam back to rain.

Travel rule for all five: walk toward what you need (home, water, a name, a crossing), not toward a compass point. Fern feels Ways as a cold seam under the sternum. False destinations smell sweet and tick **Living Tether** if followed.

## Realm Rules

Use these as table-facing truths during spirit-realm scenes:

1. **Names have weight.** Speaking a true name can reveal, wound, bind, or draw attention.
2. **Distance is associative.** Walk toward what you need, not where it is.
3. **Spirit food is forgetting.** Eating or drinking here can erode memory and identity.
4. **Heat marks the living.** Mortal souls stand out like torches to spirit predators.
5. **Crossings demand tribute.** Passage requires cost: blood, story, grief, or oath.

## Crossing Conditions

The Weft does not permit casual exit. To return, travelers need:

- A **guide or anchor** tied to boundaries (such as [[Lathun, the Drowned Path]]).
- A **stable seam** between worlds (ford, rite window, or named threshold).
- A **paid cost** that restores balance rather than stealing from it.

If these are not met, the Weft loops roads, reshapes landmarks, and keeps travelers moving in circles until they are no longer fully mortal.

## Divine and Lore Connections

- **[[Nikta, the Great Owl]]:** The Weft carries her split rhythm of sowing and reaping; imbalance appears as eternal growth without release.
- **[[Lathun, the Drowned Path]]:** Keeper of crossings, fords, and boundaries where water and world meet.
- **[[Oove, the Watcher of the Night Bloom]]:** Night-bloom eyes appear in the dark margins of the Weft, judging oathbreakers and guiding oath-keepers.
- **Red flower debt:** As noted in [[Session 14 Notes]], red flowers bridge mortal and spirit realms by spending life from both sides.

## Dangers

- Spirit predators that hunt living heat, speech, and memory.
- Echoes of unresolved rites that bind voices and replay old choices.
- Terrain that answers fear with immediate environmental shifts.
- Memory theft, identity erosion, and false paths that mirror familiar places.

## Running The Weft at the Table

- Keep scenes concrete: one vivid sensory anchor per beat.
- Reward clever naming, promises, and boundary logic over brute navigation.
- Use short clocks for identity pressure (memory loss, tether drain, pursuit).
- Let PCs bargain, but every bargain should trade one form of certainty for another.
